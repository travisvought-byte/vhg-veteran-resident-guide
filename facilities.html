Find a nursing home, rehabilitation facility or assisted living community by name, county or ZIP code, then see the bingo games, senior centers and activities near it.

Distances are approximate straight-line miles.

What this list is. It has all 924 Medicare- and Medicaid-certified nursing homes in Ohio (federal Provider Data Catalog) and 817 active, state-licensed residential care or assisted living facilities (Ohio Department of Health), both retrieved 2026-10-29. It does not identify memory care units and does not include adult day services. It does not rate or recommend any facility. Confirm services, admission and visiting rules by phone, and check the state’s Nursing Home Quality Navigator for nursing home quality information.
