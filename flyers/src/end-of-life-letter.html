VA hospice and palliative care

  • Enrolled veterans with the clinical need are eligible. No copays for hospice, from VA or a hospice under contract with VA.
  • Ask the doctor or VA social worker. Ask hospices whether they take part in We Honor Veterans.

Burial benefits (as of November 2026)

  • Service-connected death: up to $2,000. Other deaths on or after October 1, 2026: $1,032 burial and $1,032 plot allowance. Form 21P-530EZ.
  • Plan ahead: apply for a pre-need eligibility decision (VA Form 40-10007).
  • No family? VA buries unclaimed veterans with honor and helps with casket, urn and transport costs.
