Agent Orange and the PACT Act

Many nursing home veterans served in the Vietnam era. VA now presumes many of their illnesses came from service.

Scan for the full page

Conditions VA presumes from Agent Orange

Parkinson's disease and Parkinsonism; ischemic heart disease; type 2 diabetes; high blood pressure; hypothyroidism; MGUS; AL amyloidosis; bladder, prostate and respiratory cancers; multiple myeloma; Hodgkin's and non-Hodgkin's lymphoma; chronic B-cell leukemia; some soft tissue sarcomas; and others on the full page.

Where exposure is presumed

  • Vietnam 1962 to 1975, including inland waterways and ships within 12 nautical miles offshore (Blue Water Navy).
  • Thai bases 1962 to 1976; Laos; parts of Cambodia; Guam and American Samoa; Johnston Atoll; the Korean DMZ 1967 to 1971.
Denied before? File a Supplemental Claim. A surviving spouse may qualify for DIC if the veteran died of a listed condition.

Screening

  • Every veteran in VA health care gets a toxic exposure screening, repeated at least every 5 years. Ask at the next VA visit.
