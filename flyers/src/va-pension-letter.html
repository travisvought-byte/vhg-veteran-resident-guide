FOR VETERANS IN CARE AND THEIR FAMILIES

VA pension with Aid and Attendance

Monthly help for wartime veterans and surviving spouses who need care and have limited income and savings.

Scan for the full page

Who may qualify

  • Wartime service (World War II, Korea, Vietnam era, Gulf War) and not dishonorably discharged, or the surviving spouse of such a veteran.
  • Age 65 or older, disabled, or in a nursing home for long-term care because of a disability.
  • Net worth (savings and property, not the home or car, plus yearly income) under $163,699 (through November 30, 2026).

Why care costs matter

  • Out-of-pocket nursing home costs, including meals and lodging, are subtracted from the income VA counts. That can raise the payment.
  • Yearly maximum with Aid and Attendance: $29,093 for a veteran alone; $34,488 with a spouse; $18,697 for a surviving spouse (from December 1, 2025).
Medicaid pays the nursing home? A resident with no spouse or dependent child gets $90 a month in VA pension, and the facility cannot take it for the cost of care. Ask before changing any benefit.

Use only free, accredited help

  • Gifts or transfers in the 3 years before applying can stop the pension for up to 5 years.
  • Walk away from anyone who promises you will qualify, sells trusts or annuities to qualify, or charges a fee to file.
