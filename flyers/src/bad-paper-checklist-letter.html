Bad paper discharge? Start here.

Other than honorable, general or bad conduct. For the veteran and anyone helping.
Full guide
You may have more options than you were told. VA makes its own decision on your service when you apply. Military boards can upgrade a discharge. Help is free. Guide: guide.vethomeguard.org/bad-paper

This week

  • Read your DD-214. Character of service (block 24), reason and codes (blocks 25 to 28), separation date.
  • Order your complete personnel file and medical records, not only the DD-214. Free from the National Archives: vetrecs.archives.gov or form SF-180.
  • Ohio enlistee? The Ohio Dept. of Veterans Services Records Office may have your discharge papers: 877-OHIO-VET.
  • Apply to VA anyway for health care, and disability pay if you have conditions from service. VA reviews your service itself (character of discharge). Add a written statement.
  • Call one free helper: county veterans service office, accredited service officer, legal aid or a law school clinic.
  • Struggling? Do not wait on paperwork. A VA medical center will see you in an emergency whatever your discharge.

Next few weeks

  • Write your timeline: deployments, injuries, head hits, assaults, losses, when trouble started, each incident.
  • Ask for signed statements from people who knew you before, during and after service about the changes they saw.
  • Gather proof of who you are now: work, school, family, treatment, community.

Pick your board

Out less than 15 years: Discharge Review Board, form DD-293.
15 years or more, or a general court-martial: Board for Correction, form DD-149.
VA’s questions give your form and mailing address: va.gov/discharge-upgrade-instructions
  • PTSD, TBI, military sexual trauma or another mental health condition played a part? Say so. Boards must give it liberal consideration, even without a diagnosis at the time.
  • Send copies, never originals. Submit once. Keep a log: what you sent, where, when, and any docket number.
  • Denied before? New evidence, such as a new diagnosis, can be grounds to ask again. So can a lawyer you did not have at a review board.
