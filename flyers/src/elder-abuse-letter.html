FOR VETERANS IN CARE AND THEIR FAMILIES

Abuse, neglect or money being taken

You can report a concern about a veteran or older adult. You do not need proof. Many states let you report without giving your name.

Scan for the full page

What to report

  • Abuse: hitting, rough handling, threats, sexual contact without consent, isolation.
  • Neglect: missing food, medicine, hygiene or care, or a person who can no longer care for themselves.
  • Exploitation: money, property, a pension or VA benefit taken or signed away under pressure.

Where to call

  • At home: Adult Protective Services (APS) in that state. Every state's reporting route is on the full page.
  • In a nursing home or assisted living: the administrator and the long-term care ombudsman, a free advocate for residents.
  • Money stolen or a crime: also call the police.
