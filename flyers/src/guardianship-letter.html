FOR VETERANS IN CARE AND THEIR FAMILIES

Guardianship and the choices to try first

A court guardianship takes rights away. Many families can manage with less, especially if papers are signed early.

Scan for the full page

Sign while the person can

  • Financial power of attorney: an agent for money and property. In Ohio it keeps working after incapacity unless it says otherwise.
  • Health care power of attorney and living will: who decides, and what care the person wants. VA patients can use VA Form 10-0137.
  • Supported decision-making: the person decides, with trusted helpers.

VA money: the VA fiduciary

  • VA decides if a beneficiary can manage VA payments. A fiduciary handles VA benefits only; the person keeps the right to vote, sign papers and manage other money.

If guardianship is needed in Ohio

  • The county probate court decides after a hearing, on clear and convincing evidence, and must consider less restrictive options offered as evidence.
  • The person may have a lawyer, a family member or friend present and an independent evaluation. A limited guardianship can leave other rights in place.
