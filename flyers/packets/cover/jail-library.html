What the packet is

A free paper list of the first offices that help veterans, surviving spouses, older adults and people with disabilities: the county veterans office, VA health care, the Area Agency on Aging, the long-term care ombudsman, Adult Protective Services, free legal help and crisis lines. It is made for people who do not use the internet or a smartphone.

Which packet to print

  • Ohio: one packet for each county, two pages.
  • Large print: the same Ohio county contacts in 18-point type or larger.
  • Other states and D.C.: statewide contacts plus a county-by-county table.

Get them at guide.vethomeguard.org/packets.html, or scan the code above.

Calls from inside

If phone access is limited, staff can help a person send a letter or arrange an approved call. The crisis line is 988, then press 1.

How to use it

  1. Ask people whether they served. Veterans in custody can still ask the County Veterans Service Office for free help with VA claims.
  2. Print the packet for the person’s home county or state. Ohio county packets include street addresses for writing to the veterans office and county Job and Family Services.
  3. Explain the 60-day rule. A veteran held more than 60 days for a felony conviction has VA compensation reduced starting on day 61. Family members may be able to receive part of it (an apportionment). Payments can resume after release. The County Veterans Service Office can help the family ask for their share.
  4. Plan for release early. VA’s Veterans Justice Outreach and Health Care for Re-entry Veterans staff work with veterans in jails, courts and prisons. Before release, ask about VA health care enrollment and housing help: 877-424-3838, 24 hours.
  5. Civil cases only. The legal aid offices in the packet handle civil problems such as housing, benefits and family. For a criminal case, the person should ask the court or the public defender.

Privacy

The packet asks for nothing and records nothing. Veteran Home Guardians never sees who receives a packet. Each office explains its own rules when you call.

If a number here is wrong

Please tell us so we can fix it for everyone. On the website, tap Report a problem next to the listing, or email travis@vethomeguard.org and name the packet and the office. Do not include anyone’s private or medical details.
