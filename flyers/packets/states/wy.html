Help for veterans and older adults

Wyoming

The first offices to call, on paper. Asking for help costs nothing. Before you call, have the person’s name, date of birth and, for a veteran, the DD214 or service dates.

Scan for the full guide

Online: guide.vethomeguard.org/wy.html

In crisis? Call 988, then press 1 (Veterans Crisis Line) or text 838255. Anyone can call 988. In danger now: 911.

Veterans benefits and paperwork

Wyoming Veterans Commission

800-833-5987

Free help applying for VA benefits. The table below lists the veterans contact for each county.

VA health care

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs: VA Cheyenne and VA Sheridan health care systems

307-778-7550

Ask which VA medical center or clinic serves your county.

Help at home, meals and caregivers

Wyoming Department of Health, Aging Division, Community Living Section

800-442-2766

Ask for an assessment: meals, rides, help at home and caregiver support. Military service is not required.

Problems with care in a nursing home or assisted living

Wyoming Department of Health, Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program; Department of Family Services, Adult Protective Services

800-856-4398

The long-term care ombudsman works for the resident, for free.

Medicare questions

Wyoming Senior Citizens, Inc., Wyoming State Health Insurance Information Program (WSHIIP)

800-856-4398

Free, unbiased Medicare counseling.

Free legal help

Legal Aid of Wyoming

307-432-0808

For problems like eviction, benefits and debt. Not for criminal cases.

Abuse, neglect or someone taking money

Eldercare Locator

800-677-1116

Call or text. Ask for Adult Protective Services where the person lives.

National Elder Fraud Hotline

833-372-8311

10 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern, Monday to Friday.

If someone is in danger now, call 911.
