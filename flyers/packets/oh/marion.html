Help for veterans and older adults

Marion County, Ohio

The first offices to call, on paper. Asking for help costs nothing. Before you call, have the person’s name, date of birth and, for a veteran, the DD214 or service dates.

Scan for the full guide

Online: guide.vethomeguard.org/?county=Marion

In crisis? Call 988, then press 1 (Veterans Crisis Line) or text 838255. Anyone can call 988. In danger now: 911.

Veterans benefits and paperwork

Marion County Veterans Service Office

740-387-0100

Vets. Memorial Coliseum

220 E. Fairground St., Ste. 101

Marion, OH 43302

Free help applying for VA disability, pension, survivor and burial benefits. Ask about emergency financial help for veterans and their families.

VA health care

Marion VA Clinic

740-223-8089

1148 Lake Boulevard, Marion

Closest VA clinic

Dayton VA Medical Center

937-268-6511

4100 West Third Street, Dayton

VA medical center (hospital)

Sign up for VA health care: 877-222-8387. The veteran’s own VA clinic may be a different one; ask VA.

Help at home, meals and caregivers

Ohio District 5 Area Agency on Aging

800-860-5799

Area Agency on Aging. Ask for an assessment: meals, rides, help at home, caregiver support and help paying for care. Military service is not required.

Problems with care in a nursing home or assisted living

Region 5 Long-Term Care Ombudsman (Ohio District 5 Area Agency on Aging)

800-860-5799

The ombudsman works for the resident, for free. The resident decides what happens next. Ohio’s state ombudsman office: 800-282-1206.

Abuse, neglect or someone taking money

Marion County Job and Family Services

740-223-1919

363 W. Fairground St., Marion, OH 43302-1759

Ask for Adult Protective Services.

Or call Ohio’s Adult Protective Services hotline: 855-644-6277 (855-OHIO-APS). You can report without giving your name. If someone is in danger now, call 911.

Free legal help

Legal Aid of Southeast and Central Ohio (LASCO)

888-246-4420

For problems like eviction, benefits, debt, family and protection orders. Not for criminal cases.

Age 60 or older, any income: Pro Seniors legal helpline 800-488-6070.
