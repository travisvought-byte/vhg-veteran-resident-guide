For veteran-owned businesses

List your veteran-owned business free

Free profiles for veteran-owned Ohio businesses that do home work. In return you publish your rates and follow a few plain rules, so people can call you without bracing for the bill.

How it works

  1. Send us the form below.
  2. We confirm veteran ownership privately, and check your license and insurance.
  3. We send you your profile to approve.
  4. It goes live in the directory, ahead of registry-only listings.

The rules

  1. Veteran-owned, checked privately

    At least half the business is owned and run by a veteran. Show us your SBA VetCert profile, or show us your DD-214 in a way we agree on together. We write down only that we checked it and when. We never publish your DD-214 or keep a copy.

  2. Licensed and insured, and we show it

    Give us your state license number if your trade needs one (Ohio licenses commercial electrical, HVAC, plumbing, hydronics and refrigeration work), any city or county registration, and your general liability insurance certificate. Your profile shows each one with the date we checked it.

  3. Your rates, in public

    Publish your normal hourly rate, your trip or service fee and what it covers, your after-hours rate, and price ranges for three common jobs. You set your prices, not us. Customers just see them before they call.

  4. No surprises

    • Say your trip, service or diagnostic fee on the first call. Ohio law already requires it.
    • Phone prices are ranges. The firm price comes in writing after you’ve seen the job.
    • Give a written estimate before work starts.
    • Get the customer’s OK before the bill goes more than 10 percent (or $5) over the estimate.
    • Your trip fee covers travel. Don’t bill the drive again as labor.
    • Give an itemized bill: parts, new or used, labor, and who did the work.

  5. Complaints are taken seriously

    Customers can tell us about a problem. We send it to you and ask how you made it right. Breaking these rules, or a pattern of complaints that aren’t made right, takes your profile down.

  6. Free

    A profile costs nothing. Businesses we list from the public SBA registry stay labeled “public registry listing” until the owner claims them and agrees to these rules.
