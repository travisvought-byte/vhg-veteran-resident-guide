Home repair help

Find a veteran-owned contractor in Ohio

91 veteran-owned businesses in Ohio that do plumbing, heating, electrical, roofing, remodeling and other home work. Each one is certified veteran-owned by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Free to use, free to be listed.

Start here

  1. Pick your trade and area below, then call two or three businesses.
  2. On the call, ask what the trip or service fee is and what it covers. Ohio law says they must tell you at the first contact.
  3. Before work starts, ask for a written estimate. Under Ohio law the bill can’t go more than 10 percent over it (or $5, whichever is more) without your OK.
  4. Check the quote against the fair price ranges.

What the listing does and doesn’t tell you. These are public registry listings, not VHG partners or endorsements. SBA has certified that each business is veteran-owned. We have not checked their work, prices, licenses or insurance yet, and many mostly do commercial or government jobs. Some “electrical” firms do data and phone wiring, not house wiring. Ask whether they take home jobs in your area. Ohio licenses only commercial electrical, HVAC, plumbing, hydronics and refrigeration contractors; for home work, your city or county building department can tell you what registration and permits apply (Ohio Department of Commerce).
