VETERANS OUTSIDE THE U.S.

Iceland

Emergency and crisis contacts in Iceland, and help for U.S. veterans living there, each from an official source. Checked November 23, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

Emergency in Iceland

  • 112 Ambulance, fire and police

Call for immediate danger to life.

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Iceland, getting help, page dated August 11, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

No national veterans agency

We found no national veterans agency in Iceland. The Government of Iceland says Iceland is a country without a military.

Source: Government of Iceland: National security, page shows no date. Checked November 23, 2026.

Crisis and mental health lines

Red Cross Helpline 1717 (Hjálparsími Rauða krossins)

A confidential listening and support line run by the Icelandic Red Cross, listed by Neyðarlínan, Iceland’s public emergency line operator. It also offers webchat.

  • 1717 Phone

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Source: Neyðarlínan (112 Iceland): What can I do?, page shows no date. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Iceland

Veterans Crisis Line from Iceland

Iceland is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Iceland. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
