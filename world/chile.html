VETERANS OUTSIDE THE U.S.

Chile

Emergency, veterans and crisis contacts in Chile, each from an official source. Checked November 23, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

Emergency in Chile

  • 131 Ambulance
  • 132 Fire
  • 133 Police

Call for immediate danger to life.

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Chile, getting help, page dated November 9, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

Official veterans agency

Capredena (Caja de Previsión de la Defensa Nacional): armed forces pension fund

Chile’s national defence pension fund. ChileAtiende, the government’s service portal, describes it as giving pensions, health care, bonuses and other social and financial benefits to people linked to the Armed Forces. It is a pension and benefits body, not a veterans support service.

  • 600 830 6700 Call centre

Partial coverage: We did not find the call centre’s hours on an official page. This is a pension and benefits line, not a crisis line.

Hours: The ChileAtiende page we read does not give the hours.

Source: ChileAtiende (Government of Chile): queries and complaints to CAPREDENA, page shows no date. Also: ChileAtiende (Government of Chile): Capredena, page shows no date. Checked November 23, 2026.

Veterans support line

We did not find a veterans-specific crisis or support line on an official Chilean source. Use the *4141 line below.

National crisis line

*4141 suicide prevention line (Ministry of Health)

The Ministry of Health’s free suicide prevention line, answered by psychologists. Callers are referred on according to their level of risk.

  • *4141 From a mobile phone

Partial coverage: The ministry says to call *4141 from a mobile phone of any company. Its pages we read do not say whether it works from a landline. In an emergency, call 131 (ambulance) or 133 (police).

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Source: Ministry of Health (Chile): *4141 line, no estás solo, no estás sola, page shows no date. Also: Ministry of Health (Chile): how the *4141 suicide prevention line works, page dated July 13, 2025. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Chile

Veterans Crisis Line from Chile

Chile is in the U.S. Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM) region (U.S. Southern Command: Area of Responsibility, checked November 23, 2026): call +1 866-989-9599 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Chile. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
