Veterans support line

The veterans line in the card above is an information line for Malvinas war veterans, not a crisis line. We did not find a veterans-specific crisis line on an official source. Use 0800-999-0091 below.

National crisis line

National mental health urgent support line (Línea Nacional de Orientación y Apoyo en la Urgencia de Salud Mental)

A free, confidential national line for mental health emergencies, including suicide risk, published on the Ministry of Health’s pages. It can send emergency services.

  • 0800-999-0091 Phone

Hours: 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Source: Argentine Ministry of Health: national mental health 0800 line, page dated September 10, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Argentina

Veterans Crisis Line from Argentina

Argentina is in the U.S. Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM) region (U.S. Southern Command: Area of Responsibility, checked November 23, 2026): call +1 866-989-9599 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Argentina. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
