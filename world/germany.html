VETERANS OUTSIDE THE U.S.

Germany

Emergency, veterans and crisis contacts in Germany, each from an official source. Checked November 22, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

Emergency in Germany

  • 112 Ambulance and fire
  • 110 Police

Call for immediate danger to life.

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Germany, getting help, page dated October 30, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.

Official veterans agency

Veteranenbüro der Bundeswehr (Bundeswehr Veterans Office)

The central contact point for veterans of the German armed forces, focused on care and welfare. It connects each veteran with the right contacts. Fill in its contact form before you call.

  • +49 160 90 363 527 Hotline
  • +49 30 2403524 204 Office

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line.

Hours: Monday to Thursday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Friday, 8 a.m. to noon.

Address: Jean-Monnet-Straße 4, 10557 Berlin

Official website

Source: Bundeswehr: Veteranen und Veteraninnen der Bundeswehr, page dated May 2, 2025. Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans support line

Trauma-Hotline der Bundeswehr (PTSD hotline)

Free trauma line of the German armed forces, also for former members of the Bundeswehr with post-traumatic stress. The same page says benefits for a service-related injury (Wehrdienstbeschädigung) are applied for at the Bundesamt für das Personalmanagement der Bundeswehr.

  • +49 800 588 7957 Free call

Hours: 24 hours a day.

Official website

Source: Bundeswehr: PTBS-Hilfe für ehemalige Bundeswehrangehörige, page dated May 22, 2025. Checked November 22, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Germany

Veterans Crisis Line from Germany

Germany is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

Call FMP toll-free from Germany: 0800-1800-011. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:05 a.m. to 6:45 p.m. ET.

Sources: VA.gov: Foreign Medical Program, page dated October 8, 2026; for the hours, VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.
