VETERANS OUTSIDE THE U.S.

Veterans in Denmark

Living in Denmark as a veteran? Start with the emergency number, then Veterancentret (Danish Veteran Centre), the official veterans agency. If you are a U.S. veteran here, the Veterans Crisis Line and how to reach the VA Foreign Medical Program are below.

National crisis line

Livslinien (named by the Danish Health Authority)

The Danish Health Authority asks everyone who talks about suicide to refer people to Livslinien, by phone or chat at livslinien.dk.

  • 70 201 201 Phone

Partial coverage: The government page does not give Livslinien's hours, so we cannot confirm it answers around the clock. In an emergency call 112; veterans can use the 24-hour Veterancentret line above.

Hours: Not stated on the Danish Health Authority page.

U.S. veterans living in Denmark

Veterans Crisis Line from Denmark

Denmark is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Denmark. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s phone line, +1 833-930-0816, and select 3. It is a U.S. toll-free number, so it may not connect from outside the U.S. and Canada, and international charges may apply.

Source for the phone line: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.
