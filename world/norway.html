VETERANS OUTSIDE THE U.S.

Norway

Emergency, veterans and crisis contacts in Norway, each from an official source. Checked November 23, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

Emergency in Norway

  • 113 Ambulance
  • 112 Police
  • 110 Fire

Call for immediate danger to life.

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Norway, getting help, page dated August 25, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

Official veterans agency

Norwegian Armed Forces (Forsvaret): open door for veterans’ enquiries

The Norwegian Armed Forces’ telephone line for questions from veterans, listed on the City of Bergen’s help page for veterans.

  • 800 85 000 Veterans’ enquiries

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line.

Hours: Weekdays, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Official website

Source: City of Bergen (Bergen kommune): Help for veterans, page dated July 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

Veterans support line

Forsvarets grønne linje (Armed Forces green line, with Kirkens SOS)

Support for soldiers, veterans and their families, run by the Armed Forces with Kirkens SOS.

  • 800 30 445 Phone

Partial coverage: The government page gives no hours, so do not rely on it in a crisis. Use 116 123 or 113.

Hours: Not stated on the City of Bergen page.

Official website

Source: City of Bergen (Bergen kommune): Help for veterans, page dated July 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

Kameratstøttetelefonen (peer support line of the veterans’ organisation NVIO)

A nationwide line where you can talk to someone who has served abroad. You can stay anonymous. Run by a veterans’ organisation and listed by the City of Bergen.

  • 800 48 500 Phone

Hours: Staffed around the clock.

Official website

Source: City of Bergen (Bergen kommune): Help for veterans, page dated July 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

National crisis line

Mental Helse helpline and Kirkens SOS (listed by Helsenorge)

Helsenorge, the Norwegian public health portal, lists these 24-hour lines for anyone who needs someone to talk to. In acute risk of suicide, call 113.

  • 116 123 Mental Helse helpline
  • 22 40 00 40 Kirkens SOS

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Official website

Source: Helsenorge: Do you need someone to talk to?, page dated March 8, 2024. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Norway

Veterans Crisis Line from Norway

Norway is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Norway. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
