Crisis and mental health lines on Okinawa

Kokoro no Denwa mental health line (こころの電話相談), Okinawa Prefecture

Telephone mental health consultation for people living in Okinawa, run by the Okinawa Prefectural Comprehensive Mental Health and Welfare Center. The prefecture’s page is in Japanese.

  • 098-888-1450 Consultation line

Partial coverage: Weekday daytime hours only, and closed on Tuesdays. It is not a 24-hour line. For a 24-hour line, see Japan’s national lines on the Japan page; in an emergency, call 119.

Hours: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, 9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 4 p.m.; closed Tuesdays, weekends, public holidays, the New Year holidays and Okinawa Memorial Day (Irei no Hi).

Source: Okinawa Prefecture: mental health and welfare consultation (精神保健福祉相談, in Japanese), page dated May 14, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

Okinawa Psychiatric Emergency Medical Information Center (精神科救急医療情報センター)

Okinawa Prefecture’s after-hours consultation line for psychiatric emergencies. The prefecture’s page is in Japanese.

  • 098-889-8893 Nights and days off

Partial coverage: Nights and days off only. On weekday daytimes, use the Kokoro no Denwa line above. In a life-threatening emergency, call 119.

Hours: Weekdays, 5 p.m. to 9 a.m. the next morning; on days off (休日), 9 a.m. to 9 a.m. the next morning.

Source: Okinawa Prefecture: Psychiatric Emergency Medical Information Center (in Japanese), page dated March 6, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Okinawa

Veterans Crisis Line from Okinawa

Okinawa is in the U.S. Indo-Pacific (PACOM) region: call +1 844-702-5493 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

Call FMP toll-free from Japan, including Okinawa: 00531-13-0871. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:05 a.m. to 6:45 p.m. ET.

Sources: VA.gov: Foreign Medical Program, page dated October 8, 2026; for the hours, VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.
