The ministry directorate for veterans (gaziler) and the families of service members killed on duty: their rights, identity cards and employment. It is in Ankara.

Partial coverage: A switchboard in Ankara; the page gives no hours. This is not a crisis line.

Veterans support line

ALO 183 social support line (Ministry of Family and Social Services)

The ministry’s free line for guidance and counselling on services for veterans and their families and for the families of those killed in service, as well as for families, women, children, disabled and older people. It also takes reports of neglect, abuse and violence.

  • 183 Free from within Turkey

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Source: Ministry of Family and Social Services: About ALO 183, page shows no date. Also: Ministry of Family and Social Services: What is ALO 183?, page shows no date. Checked November 23, 2026.

National crisis line

112: general emergency number (no national suicide crisis line found)

We did not find a national suicide or mental health crisis line on an official Turkish source. In a mental health emergency, call 112. ALO 183 above offers counselling around the clock but is not a crisis line.

  • 112 Emergency

Partial coverage: No crisis line: this is the general emergency number, listed because we found no national crisis line on an official source.

Hours: 24 hours a day.

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Turkey, getting help, page dated October 27, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Turkey

Veterans Crisis Line from Turkey

Turkey is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Turkey. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
