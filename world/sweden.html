Veterans in Sweden

Living in Sweden as a veteran? Start with the emergency number, then Swedish Armed Forces Veterans Centre (Försvarsmaktens veterancentrum), the official veterans agency. If you are a U.S. veteran here, the Veterans Crisis Line and how to reach the VA Foreign Medical Program are below.

Official veterans agency

Swedish Armed Forces Veterans Centre (Försvarsmaktens veterancentrum)

The Swedish Armed Forces’ centre for veteran and family matters. Call with questions about its work, or for help finding the right unit and its veteran and family coordinator.

  • 072-388 04 27 Veterans Centre
  • 08-788 75 00 Armed Forces switchboard

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line.

Hours: Veterans Centre: weekdays, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Email: veterancentrum@mil.se

National crisis line

Mind Självmordslinjen and 1177 (listed by 1177 Vårdguiden)

1177, the public health care service, lists the Mind suicide line for anyone with thoughts of suicide, and its own 1177 number for health care advice around the clock. In an urgent situation, call 112.

  • 90101 Mind Självmordslinjen
  • 1177 Health care advice

Partial coverage: 1177 does not give the suicide line’s hours, so we cannot confirm it answers around the clock. 1177 itself answers day and night; in an emergency call 112.

Hours: 1177: around the clock. Självmordslinjen: not stated on the 1177 page.

Official website

Source: 1177: To you who have suicidal thoughts, page dated March 26, 2024. Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Sweden

Veterans Crisis Line from Sweden

Sweden is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Sweden. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s phone line, +1 833-930-0816, and select 3. It is a U.S. toll-free number, so it may not connect from outside the U.S. and Canada, and international charges may apply.

Source for the phone line: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.
