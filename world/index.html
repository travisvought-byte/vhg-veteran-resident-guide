VA Foreign Medical Program (FMP)

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

Claims can be filed online, by email to HAC.FMP@va.gov, or by mail to VHA Office of Integrated Veteran Care, Foreign Medical Program (FMP), PO Box 200, Spring City, PA 19475.

How we check

Every number comes from a government body, a national public body, the WHO or another UN agency. A charity appears only when a government page names it, and we cite that government page. For emergency numbers we also accept the UK and Australian governments’ travel advice. We never guess a number. Each listing shows its source and the date we checked it. We did not telephone the agencies.
