Veterans Crisis Line from outside the U.S.

For U.S. veterans, by region. If your number does not work, try another, or use online chat. International charges may apply.

  • North America (NORTHCOM): Dial 988 then press 1
  • Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493 off base, DSN 988 on base
  • Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495 off base, DSN 988 on base
  • Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719 off base, DSN 988 on base
  • Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054 off base, DSN 988 on base
  • Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599 off base, DSN 988 on base

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.
