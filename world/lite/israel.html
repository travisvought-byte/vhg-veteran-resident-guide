Official veterans agency

Ministry of Defense Rehabilitation Department (אגף השיקום)

Recognition and rehabilitation for disabled veterans of the Israel Defense Forces. Call the service center about a claim or your benefits.

*8150 Service center 03-7776777 Service center

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line. Hours: Sunday to Thursday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Website: shikum.mod.gov.il

Source: Ministry of Defense Rehabilitation Department: Contact us (shikum.mod.gov.il). Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans support line

Nefesh Achat (נפש אחת) support line

For disabled IDF veterans coping with post-traumatic stress, people who have applied for disability recognition, released soldiers coping with post-traumatic stress, and family members of people struggling after their service.

*8944 Phone

Hours: 7 days a week, 24 hours a day, including holidays.

Website: shikum.mod.gov.il

U.S. veterans living in Israel

Veterans Crisis Line: Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM)

Call +1 855-422-7719 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line from Israel. Use Ask VA online, or call +1 833-930-0816 and select 3 (a U.S. number that may not connect from abroad; charges may apply).

Source: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim (va.gov). Checked November 23, 2026.
