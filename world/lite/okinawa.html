Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Japan, getting help (gov.uk). Checked November 23, 2026.

Okinawa is a prefecture of Japan. See also Japan.

National crisis line

Kokoro no Denwa mental health line (こころの電話相談), Okinawa Prefecture

Telephone mental health consultation for people living in Okinawa, run by the Okinawa Prefectural Comprehensive Mental Health and Welfare Center. The prefecture’s page is in Japanese.

098-888-1450 Consultation line

Partial coverage: Weekday daytime hours only, and closed on Tuesdays. It is not a 24-hour line. For a 24-hour line, see Japan’s national lines on the Japan page; in an emergency, call 119. Hours: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, 9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 4 p.m.; closed Tuesdays, weekends, public holidays, the New Year holidays and Okinawa Memorial Day (Irei no Hi).

Source: Okinawa Prefecture: mental health and welfare consultation (精神保健福祉相談, in Japanese) (pref.okinawa.jp). Checked November 23, 2026.

Okinawa Psychiatric Emergency Medical Information Center (精神科救急医療情報センター)

Okinawa Prefecture’s after-hours consultation line for psychiatric emergencies. The prefecture’s page is in Japanese.

098-889-8893 Nights and days off

Partial coverage: Nights and days off only. On weekday daytimes, use the Kokoro no Denwa line above. In a life-threatening emergency, call 119. Hours: Weekdays, 5 p.m. to 9 a.m. the next morning; on days off (休日), 9 a.m. to 9 a.m. the next morning.

Source: Okinawa Prefecture: Psychiatric Emergency Medical Information Center (in Japanese) (pref.okinawa.jp). Checked November 23, 2026.

Foster Retired Activities Office (Marine Corps Community Services Okinawa)

The Retired Activities Office at Camp Foster links retired service members, their families and survivors with the help available to them on Okinawa.

098-970-3159 Commercial, from a phone in Japan

Address: Building 445, Camp Foster.

Hours: Monday and Tuesday, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.; closed Wednesday to Sunday.

DSN: 645-3159.

Email: MCBB_TRP@usmc.mil

Source: Marine Corps Community Services Okinawa: Retired Affairs (okinawa.usmc-mccs.org). Checked November 23, 2026.
