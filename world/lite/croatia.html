Veterans in Croatia

Emergency in Croatia: call 112 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Croatia, getting help (gov.uk). Checked November 23, 2026.

Official veterans agency

Ministry of Croatian Veterans (Ministarstvo hrvatskih branitelja)

The national ministry for veterans of the Homeland War and their families. Call the switchboard, or the ministry’s own numbers for psychosocial and health matters. The government also lists a psychosocial help centre and a ministry office in every county.

01 2308 888 Switchboard 01 2308 807 Psychosocial and health matters 01 2308 993 Psychosocial and health matters

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line. Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Email: ministarstvo@branitelji.hr

Address: Trg Nevenke Topalušić 1, 10000 Zagreb

Website: branitelji.gov.hr

Source: Ministry of Croatian Veterans: Contacts (branitelji.gov.hr). Checked November 23, 2026.

Veterans support line

We did not find a veterans-specific crisis line on an official Croatian source. The ministry’s psychosocial numbers above answer in office hours. For a 24-hour line, use the crisis centre below.

National crisis line

Centre for Crisis Situations and Suicide Prevention, University Hospital Centre Zagreb (listed by the Ministry of Justice)

The crisis and suicide prevention centre of Zagreb’s university hospital, listed by the Ministry of Justice among useful telephone numbers. The number is in Zagreb (area code 01).

01 2376 470 Phone

Hours: Every day, around the clock (0 to 24 hours).

Website: mpudt.gov.hr

Source: Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Croatia: Useful telephone numbers (mpudt.gov.hr). Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Croatia

Veterans Crisis Line: Europe (EUCOM)

Call +1 844-702-5495 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line from Croatia. Use Ask VA online, or call +1 833-930-0816 and select 3 (a U.S. number that may not connect from abroad; charges may apply).

Source: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim (va.gov). Checked November 23, 2026.
