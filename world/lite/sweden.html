Veterans in Sweden

Emergency in Sweden: call 112 (Ambulance, fire and police).

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Sweden, getting help (gov.uk). Checked November 23, 2026.

Official veterans agency

Swedish Armed Forces Veterans Centre (Försvarsmaktens veterancentrum)

The Swedish Armed Forces’ centre for veteran and family matters. Call with questions about its work, or for help finding the right unit and its veteran and family coordinator.

072-388 04 27 Veterans Centre 08-788 75 00 Armed Forces switchboard

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line. Hours: Veterans Centre: weekdays, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Email: veterancentrum@mil.se

Website: forsvarsmakten.se

Source: Swedish Armed Forces: Contacts and resources for veterans and relatives (forsvarsmakten.se). Checked November 23, 2026.

Veterans support line

Sveriges veteranförbund on-call line (listed by the Swedish Armed Forces)

Peer support by phone from the Swedish veterans’ association, listed on the Armed Forces’ page on support in a crisis.

020-666 333 Phone

Partial coverage: Evenings only, 6 to 9 p.m. daily. In an emergency call 112. Hours: Daily, 6 to 9 p.m.

Website: forsvarsmakten.se

Source: Swedish Armed Forces: Support and help for veterans (forsvarsmakten.se). Checked November 23, 2026.

Svenska Soldathemsförbundet support line (listed by the Swedish Armed Forces)

Professional conversation support for individuals, couples or families, from the soldiers’ homes federation.

070-570 99 91 Phone

Partial coverage: Daytime and evening only, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. daily. Hours: Daily, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Website: forsvarsmakten.se

Source: Swedish Armed Forces: Support and help for veterans (forsvarsmakten.se). Checked November 23, 2026.

National crisis line

Mind Självmordslinjen and 1177 (listed by 1177 Vårdguiden)

1177, the public health care service, lists the Mind suicide line for anyone with thoughts of suicide, and its own 1177 number for health care advice around the clock. In an urgent situation, call 112.

90101 Mind Självmordslinjen 1177 Health care advice

Partial coverage: 1177 does not give the suicide line’s hours, so we cannot confirm it answers around the clock. 1177 itself answers day and night; in an emergency call 112. Hours: 1177: around the clock. Självmordslinjen: not stated on the 1177 page.

Website: 1177.se

Source: 1177: To you who have suicidal thoughts (1177.se). Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Sweden

Veterans Crisis Line: Europe (EUCOM)

Call +1 844-702-5495 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line from Sweden. Use Ask VA online, or call +1 833-930-0816 and select 3 (a U.S. number that may not connect from abroad; charges may apply).

Source: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim (va.gov). Checked November 23, 2026.

VA benefits questions

+1 412-395-6272, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern.

Source: VA: Veterans Living Abroad Contact Information (benefits.va.gov). Checked November 22, 2026.
