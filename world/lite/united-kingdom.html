Official veterans agency

Veterans UK (Ministry of Defence)

Free support for veterans and their families: a helpline, Veterans Services, armed forces pensions, and injury and bereavement compensation payments. Call or email to ask about benefits or how to apply.

0808 1914 218 Freephone, UK only +44 1253 866 043 From overseas 0800 085 3600 Armed forces pensions

Partial coverage: Office hours only. This is not a crisis line. Hours: Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Email: veterans-uk@mod.gov.uk

Website: gov.uk

Source: GOV.UK: Veterans UK (gov.uk). Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans support line

Op COURAGE: NHS veterans mental health service (England)

The NHS mental health and wellbeing service for veterans and reservists in England. You can contact it yourself.

0300 373 3332 North of England 0300 323 0137 Midlands 0300 034 9991 East of England 020 3317 6818 London 0300 365 2000 (option 4) South East England 0300 034 9986 South West England

Partial coverage: England only, by region. The NHS page gives no hours, so do not rely on it in a crisis. Hours: Not stated on the NHS page.

Website: nhs.uk

Source: NHS: Mental health support for veterans, service leavers and reservists (nhs.uk). Checked November 22, 2026.

National crisis line

Samaritans (listed by the UK government) and NHS 111

The UK government's Urgent help for veterans page lists Samaritans for anyone struggling, any time, and NHS 111 in England and Scotland for urgent mental health help.

116 123 Samaritans, freephone 111 NHS 111 (England and Scotland)

Hours: Samaritans: 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Website: gov.uk

Source: GOV.UK: Urgent help for veterans (gov.uk). Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans Crisis Line: Europe (EUCOM)

Call +1 844-702-5495 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

Toll-free from United Kingdom: 0800-032-7425. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:05 a.m. to 6:45 p.m. ET.

Source: VA.gov: Foreign Medical Program (va.gov). Checked November 22, 2026.
