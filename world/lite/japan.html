Veterans in Japan

Emergency in Japan: call 110 (Police), 119 (Ambulance and fire).

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: Japan, getting help (gov.uk). Checked November 23, 2026.

Has its own page: Okinawa.

No national veterans agency

We found no national veterans agency in Japan on an official source. Use the emergency and crisis numbers below. U.S. veterans living in Japan can use the VA help further down.

Checked November 23, 2026.

National crisis line

Yorisoi Hotline (よりそいホットライン)

A free 24-hour listening and support line, run by the Social Inclusion Support Center and listed by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. The ministry’s page is in Japanese.

0120-279-338 Free call 050-3655-0279 From an IP phone starting 050

Hours: 24 hours a day.

Website: mhlw.go.jp

Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Japan): telephone consultation lines (まもろうよ こころ, in Japanese) (mhlw.go.jp). Checked November 23, 2026.

Kokoro no Kenkō Sōdan Unified Dial (こころの健康相談統一ダイヤル)

One number that connects you to the public mental health consultation service of the prefecture or designated city you call from, listed by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. It cannot be reached from IP phones starting 050.

0570-064-556 Navi Dial (call charges apply)

Partial coverage: Days and hours differ by prefecture. It is not a 24-hour line everywhere.

Website: mhlw.go.jp

Source: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Japan): telephone consultation lines (まもろうよ こころ, in Japanese) (mhlw.go.jp). Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Japan

Veterans Crisis Line: Indo-Pacific (PACOM)

Call +1 844-702-5493 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

Toll-free from Japan: 00531-13-0871. Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:05 a.m. to 6:45 p.m. ET.

Source: VA.gov: Foreign Medical Program (va.gov). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA benefits questions

+1 412-395-6272, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern.

Source: VA: Veterans Living Abroad Contact Information (benefits.va.gov). Checked November 22, 2026.
