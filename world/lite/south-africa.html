Veterans in South Africa

Emergency in South Africa: call 10177 (Ambulance and fire), 10111 (Police), 112 (All emergencies from mobiles).

Source: GOV.UK travel advice: South Africa, getting help (gov.uk). Checked November 23, 2026.

Veterans support line

We did not find a veterans-specific crisis or support line on an official South African source. Use the suicide crisis line below.

National crisis line

SADAG Suicide Crisis Line (named by the South African Government News Agency)

The South African Depression and Anxiety Group’s suicide crisis line, given by the government news agency. You can also SMS 31393.

0800 567 567 Phone

Partial coverage: The government article gives no hours. In immediate danger, call 10177, or 112 from a mobile. Hours: Not stated in the government article.

Source: SAnews (South African Government News Agency): Call for investment in psychosocial support as suicide rates increase (sanews.gov.za). Checked November 23, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in South Africa

Veterans Crisis Line: Africa (AFRICOM)

Call +1 888-482-6054 off base, or DSN 988 on base. International charges may apply.

If that number does not work, use online chat or another regional number on the list of all regions.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas (veteranscrisisline.net). Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care abroad: the Foreign Medical Program

Pays for care abroad for a VA-rated, service-connected condition. Get your FMP benefits letter on VA.gov under Letters.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line from South Africa. Use Ask VA online, or call +1 833-930-0816 and select 3 (a U.S. number that may not connect from abroad; charges may apply).

Source: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim (va.gov). Checked November 23, 2026.
