U.S. veterans living in Canada

Veterans Crisis Line from Canada

For North America (NORTHCOM): the Veterans Crisis Line lists “Dial 988 then press 1”.

In Canada, 988 reaches Canada's own 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline, which helps anyone in Canada. To reach the U.S. Veterans Crisis Line itself, use online chat, or try another regional number below.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

Call FMP at 1-877-345-8179 (U.S. and Canada, toll-free) or +1 303-331-7590.

The current FMP page shows a "U.S. and Canada" line, but its number did not appear in the page text we read. These two numbers come from VA's FMP Guide, revised April 5, 2023: 1-877-345-8179 toll-free in the United States and Canada, and 303-331-7590 as the program's direct phone.

VA's FMP Guide (revised April 5, 2023) says inquiries and claims from veterans living in Canada go to Foreign Countries Operations, Ottawa: toll-free 1-888-996-2242. Confirm this with FMP before you send a claim.

Source: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
