U.S. veterans living in Malaysia

Veterans Crisis Line from Malaysia

Malaysia is in the U.S. Indo-Pacific (PACOM) region: call +1 844-702-5493 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Europe (EUCOM): +1 844-702-5495, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Malaysia. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s direct number, +1 303-331-7590 (international charges apply).

Source for the direct number: VA: Foreign Medical Program Guide (PDF), page dated April 5, 2023. Checked November 22, 2026.
