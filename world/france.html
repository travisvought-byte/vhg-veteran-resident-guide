Official veterans agency

Office national des combattants et des victimes de guerre (ONaCVG)

A public body of the Ministry of the Armed Forces. It protects the interests of veterans and war victims, issues the combatant's card (carte du combattant) and gives social help. Each département has its own ONaCVG service; the Paris service is shown here.

  • 01 44 41 38 10 Paris departmental service

Partial coverage: Office hours only, one office per département. This is not a crisis line.

Hours: Weekday mornings 9 a.m. to noon; also Tuesday and Thursday 1:30 to 4 p.m. (Paris service).

Email: sd75@onacvg.fr

Official website

Source: Service-Public.fr directory: ONaCVG, Paris departmental service, page dated November 4, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans support line

Écoute Défense

Clinical psychologists of the armed forces health service listen and direct you to follow-up care. Anonymous and free. For military personnel and their families; the Ministry of the Armed Forces also lists it for former reservists coping with an injury or illness.

  • 08 08 800 321 Free from landlines and mobiles

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Official website

Source: Ministry of the Armed Forces (Maison numérique des blessés): After my injury or illness, page dated June 2, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in France

Veterans Crisis Line from France

France is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for France. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s phone line, +1 833-930-0816, and select 3. It is a U.S. toll-free number, so it may not connect from outside the U.S. and Canada, and international charges may apply.

Source for the phone line: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.
