Veterans in the Netherlands

Living in the Netherlands as a veteran? Start with the emergency number, then Veteranenloket (Veterans Desk), the official veterans agency. If you are a U.S. veteran here, the Veterans Crisis Line and how to reach the VA Foreign Medical Program are below.

Official veterans agency

Veteranenloket (Veterans Desk)

For veterans, war and service victims and their relatives. It provides care and services from the Ministry of Defence, and is the entry point to the national veterans care network (Landelijk Zorgsysteem Veteranen). Military pensions are handled by ABP.

  • 088 334 00 00 Veteranenloket
  • 045 579 62 55 ABP military pensions

Hours: Veteranenloket: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Email: info@veteranenloket.nl

Official website

Source: Ministry of Defence (Defensie): Contact for veterans, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

Veterans support line

The Veteranenloket number above is the veterans support line: the Ministry of Defence lists it as open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

National crisis line

113 Zelfmoordpreventie (suicide prevention)

Telephone help line for people with thoughts of suicide, funded by the Dutch government. Anonymous and free.

  • 113 Phone
  • 0800 0113 Free phone

Hours: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Official website

Source: Rijksoverheid (Government of the Netherlands): Reducing suicides, page dated May 26, 2026. Checked November 22, 2026.

U.S. veterans living in Netherlands

Veterans Crisis Line from Netherlands

Netherlands is in the U.S. Europe (EUCOM) region (U.S. European Command: The region, checked November 22, 2026): call +1 844-702-5495 off base or DSN 988 on base.

If that does not work, the Veterans Crisis Line says to try another regional number (Indo-Pacific (PACOM): +1 844-702-5493, Middle East and Central Asia (CENTCOM): +1 855-422-7719, Africa (AFRICOM): +1 888-482-6054, Central and South America and the Caribbean (SOUTHCOM): +1 866-989-9599), or use online chat. International charges may apply.

Source: Veterans Crisis Line: Military Crisis Line and calling from overseas, page shows no date. Checked November 22, 2026.

VA health care: the Foreign Medical Program

Medically necessary care outside the United States for a VA-rated, service-connected disability, for a condition associated with and held to be aggravating a service-connected disability, or as part of Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E). FMP does not cover care or supplies received in or from the U.S. or U.S. territories.

Veterans with a VA-rated, service-connected condition can get an FMP benefits letter by signing in to VA.gov and opening Letters from the My VA menu. Providers can use the letter to file claims. If you already paid for care, you can file the claim yourself.

VA lists no toll-free FMP line for Netherlands. You can reach FMP online through Ask VA, or call the program’s phone line, +1 833-930-0816, and select 3. It is a U.S. toll-free number, so it may not connect from outside the U.S. and Canada, and international charges may apply.

Source for the phone line: VA.gov: How to file a VA Foreign Medical Program claim, page dated October 29, 2026. Checked November 23, 2026.
