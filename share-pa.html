Ready-to-use newsletter introduction

Veteran Home Guardians has created a free Pennsylvania resource guide for veterans, surviving spouses, seniors, people with disabilities, families and care teams. Choose any of Pennsylvania’s 67 counties to find county veterans office contacts, matched local aging referrals and local or statewide ombudsman routes. The guide also includes ramps and home accessibility funding routes, non-veteran senior support, and printable referral tools. Explore the guide at https://travisvought-byte.github.io/vhg-veteran-resident-guide/pa.html. Please share it with someone who could use a starting point.
