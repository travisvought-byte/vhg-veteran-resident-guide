Veteran Home Guardians · Working pilot, not an emergency service

Pilot status: This is an early resource-matching tool, not yet an AI chatbot. It reads published guide records and prepares a response you can copy. It does not send email, save personal details or decide eligibility. A human should review recommendations before relying on them.

Tell us what kind of help is needed

Do not include names, addresses, Social Security numbers, medical histories, claim numbers, or other private information. Location and a general description are enough.

Urgent support

For immediate danger, call 911. For suicidal thoughts or emotional crisis, call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. Do not wait for an email reply.
