VA Monthly Training Allowance for Paralympic and Olympic Athletes

Benefits · U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, National Veterans Sports Programs · Open in every state

A monthly allowance, paid at the VR&E Chapter 31 rate, for veterans with disabilities who train and compete in Paralympic sports (or Olympic sports with a 30%+ service-connected rating).

Who can apply
Veterans under 38 U.S.C. 101 not barred under 38 CFR 3.12. Paralympic: classified as eligible by the sport federation and meeting VA's performance standard. How to apply
Gather your VA Benefits Summary Letter, meet the published performance standard for your sport, and submit VA Form 10091 through the FSC Customer Engagement Portal. Questions: stipends4vets@va.gov.
