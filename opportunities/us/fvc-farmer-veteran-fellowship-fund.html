Farmer Veteran Fellowship Fund

Farmer Veteran Coalition · Open in every state

Business · Farmer Veteran Coalition · Open in every state

Small grants of $1,000 to $5,000 for veterans in their first years of farming or ranching. The money is paid directly to approved vendors for an item the veteran needs for the farm business, such as fencing, breeding livestock, tractor implements or beekeeping equipment.

Who can apply
Farmer Veteran Coalition members who served or are serving in any U.S. military branch, any era; honorable discharge (other characterizations considered case by case); currently serving members need a support letter from their commander. Must have an agricultural business in operation and a business plan (nonprofits submit a strategic plan). The official pages conflict on nonprofits: the program page says for-profit only, while the FAQ says nonprofits may apply but for-profit farms get priority. No location, years-farming or disability requirement is stated. Prior recipients: the program page allows reapplying only if the earlier award was $1,000 or less; the FAQ says former recipients cannot reapply.
How to apply
Join the Farmer Veteran Coalition, then apply online when the yearly application opens (it stays open at least four weeks). The form has short-answer and essay questions plus uploads of a business plan of up to five pages and proof of service; an advisory panel reviews applications.
Deadline or date
The 2026 cycle is complete (awardees announced May 1, 2026). The application opens once a year for at least four weeks; review is in March and awards come in spring. Next cycle dates are not announced.
Where
Online
Cost
Free to apply (no fee stated); Farmer Veteran Coalition membership required
Award
$1,000 to $5,000, paid to vendors for one farm item (not paid to the veteran)
Takes equity
Not stated by the program

How many it serves and the odds

  • Serves: More than 930 veterans funded and nearly $4 million awarded since 2011
  • Odds: not published. The program does not say how many apply.

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
