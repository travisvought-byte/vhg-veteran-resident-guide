Hiring Our Heroes Small Business Grant

Business · Hiring Our Heroes, U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation (supported by the FedEx Founders Fund) · Open in every state

One-time cash grants for small businesses owned by veterans and military spouses: four grants of $10,000 and one grand prize of $25,000 in the 2027 program. Winners are announced at a Hiring Our Heroes event in spring 2027.

Who can apply
The applicant must own 51% or more of a U.S.-based, for-profit business and be an honorably discharged veteran (active duty, Guard or Reserve), a current Guard or Reserve member, or a current military spouse, and legally live in the 48 contiguous states, Alaska or Hawaii. The business must have 3 to 20 workers (counting owners, full- and part-time staff and 1099 contractors) and under $5 million in annual revenue, and be in a zip code with a Distressed Communities Index score of 40 or higher or show financial need from hardship beyond the owner's control. Nonprofits, government entities, large franchises (over 25 corporate-owned or 50 total stores), gas stations, direct sellers, and businesses mainly in cannabis, gambling, tobacco or e-cigarettes, firearms, diet aids, religious instruction or adult content are excluded. Years in business are not stated.
How to apply
Apply online through the Apply Now form on the Hiring Our Heroes Small Business Grant page; only online entries count. The form has eligibility questions, business details and five short essays (about 250 words each). Winners must later provide proof of service, business photos and a headshot.
Deadline or date
Applications for the 2027 program are due by 11:59 p.m. EDT, December 15, 2026. Winners are notified by March 29, 2027.
Where
Online; winners announced at a Hiring Our Heroes event in spring 2027 (place not published)
Cost
Free (no entry fee stated)
Award
Up to $25,000: four grants of $10,000 and one grand prize of $25,000
Takes equity
Not stated by the program

Apply or learn more about Hiring Our Heroes Small Business Grant on hiringourheroes.org

How many it serves and the odds

  • Places: 5 grants per cycle (four $10,000 and one $25,000), chosen from up to 20 semifinalists
  • Odds: not published. The program does not say how many apply.

Military Spouse Scholarship for spouse-owned businesses

Business · National Military Family Association (NMFA) · Open in every state

NMFA's military spouse scholarship can also be used for a military spouse-owned business. Funding can cover business needs including inventory and merchandise, business marketing, branding, manufacturing, supplies and permits.

Who can apply
The spouse of a service member who served after 9/11/2001 and is active duty, retired (including medically retired), a 100% disabled veteran, Guard, Reserve, or a fallen service member, in any of the 8 Uniformed Services (Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard, USPHS Commissioned Corps, NOAA). The spouse must have, or be eligible to hold, a valid military or Uniformed Services ID. The business must be military spouse-owned. Veterans themselves cannot apply, and spouses of veterans who are neither retired nor rated 100% disabled do not qualify. NMFA volunteers, employees and board members are not eligible. The page sets no revenue, employee or business-age limits and does not address nonprofits.
How to apply
Apply online through NMFA's spouse scholarship site, which says applications are open. Submit a new application each scholarship year (October 1 to September 30). There is no limit on how many times you can apply and receive funding.
Deadline or date
Rolling, open year-round; a new application is needed each scholarship year (Oct 1 to Sep 30). Winners are announced in three rounds, by the last day of February, June and October 2027.
Where
Online
Cost
No application fee published
Award
One-time award, averaging $500 to $5,000 per the FAQ; the scholarship home page says $500 to $1,000 on average. Paid by check to the program or vendor address you give, or in some cases as a reimbursement with receipts.
Takes equity
Not stated by the program

Apply or learn more about Military Spouse Scholarship for spouse-owned businesses on scholarships.militaryfamily.org

How many it serves and the odds

  • Serves: 662 military spouses selected for a scholarship in 2025, totaling $833,675 (all scholarship uses, not business only)
  • Applicants: 9,816 applicants in 2025 (all scholarship uses, not business only)
  • Odds: not published. The program does not say how many apply.

Figures from militaryfamily.org, 2025.

DAV Patriots Pitch

Business · DAV (Disabled American Veterans), DAV Patriot Boot Camp · Worth the trip: DAV National Headquarters near Cincinnati or other U.S. cities (varies by cohort)

A Shark Tank-style pitch contest where veteran and military-spouse entrepreneurs selected from each DAV Patriot Boot Camp cohort pitch a panel of veteran investors and experts for non-dilutive funding. The boot camp itself is a free three-day in-person program with training, mentoring and networking.

Who can apply
Pitch contestants are selected from each DAV Patriot Boot Camp cohort; there is no open application for the pitch itself. The boot camp is for veterans, military-connected community members and spouses who own or founded a for-profit business that is scalable and looking to grow; franchisees and nonprofits are not eligible. An EIN, a website, and revenue or capital raised in the past year are preferred but not required. No service-connected disability requirement is stated.
How to apply
By invitation only. Register for a DAV Patriot Boot Camp cohort through the Apply Now button on the Patriot Boot Camp page; seats are first come, first served, subject to eligibility. Entrepreneurs from each cohort are then selected to compete in DAV Patriots Pitch.
Deadline or date
Invite only from boot camp cohorts. Registration for the Winter 2027 DAV Patriot Boot Camp is listed with an Apply Now button, but dates, place and deadline are not yet published. Recent pitch events were in July 2026 (DAV National Convention) and October 2026 (Alumni Accelerator).
Where
DAV National Headquarters near Cincinnati or other U.S. cities (varies by cohort)
Travel
In-person three-day boot camps at DAV National Headquarters near Cincinnati or other cities. DAV covers programming, meals and event transportation for those staying at partner hotels, plus a ride back to the airport and parking; participants pay their own travel to the host city, lodging and incidentals.
Cost
Free (participants pay their own travel and lodging)
Award
Thousands of dollars in non-dilutive funding; exact amounts are not published on the official pages
Takes equity
No. The program says it takes no equity.
Not confirmed
A $10,000 prize was reported in local news about one 2026 alumni event; DAV's own pages say only thousands of dollars.

Farmer Veteran Fellowship Fund

Business · Farmer Veteran Coalition · Open in every state

Small grants of $1,000 to $5,000 for veterans in their first years of farming or ranching. The money is paid directly to approved vendors for an item the veteran needs for the farm business, such as fencing, breeding livestock, tractor implements or beekeeping equipment.

Who can apply
Farmer Veteran Coalition members who served or are serving in any U.S. military branch, any era; honorable discharge (other characterizations considered case by case); currently serving members need a support letter from their commander. Must have an agricultural business in operation and a business plan (nonprofits submit a strategic plan). The official pages conflict on nonprofits: the program page says for-profit only, while the FAQ says nonprofits may apply but for-profit farms get priority. No location, years-farming or disability requirement is stated. Prior recipients: the program page allows reapplying only if the earlier award was $1,000 or less; the FAQ says former recipients cannot reapply.
How to apply
Join the Farmer Veteran Coalition, then apply online when the yearly application opens (it stays open at least four weeks). The form has short-answer and essay questions plus uploads of a business plan of up to five pages and proof of service; an advisory panel reviews applications.
Deadline or date
The 2026 cycle is complete (awardees announced May 1, 2026). The application opens once a year for at least four weeks; review is in March and awards come in spring. Next cycle dates are not announced.
Where
Online
Cost
Free to apply (no fee stated); Farmer Veteran Coalition membership required
Award
$1,000 to $5,000, paid to vendors for one farm item (not paid to the veteran)
Takes equity
Not stated by the program

Apply or learn more about Farmer Veteran Fellowship Fund on farmvetco.org

How many it serves and the odds

  • Serves: More than 930 veterans funded and nearly $4 million awarded since 2011
  • Odds: not published. The program does not say how many apply.

TWU Veteran Entrepreneur Grant

Business · Texas Woman's University, Center for Women Entrepreneurs (Jane Nelson Institute) · Anywhere in Texas

Five $5,000 grants a year for veteran-owned small businesses in Texas to fund a growth project such as equipment, technology, inventory, property improvements or marketing. Winners complete a virtual small business training series, a business plan and three hours with a small business advisor.

Who can apply
A for-profit business (corporation, partnership, LLP, LLC or sole proprietorship) already formed, located and operating in Texas, at least 51% owned by one or more veterans who are U.S. citizens or permanent residents (honorable or general under honorable conditions discharge, transitioning active duty with a set discharge date, or Reserve or Guard). Nonprofits are not eligible. Owners must be current on taxes and licenses, have no pending litigation and no felony or dishonesty convictions. Prior CWE grant winners and anyone in a CWE program in the past year cannot apply. The pages conflict on women-only: the FAQ says any veteran-owned Texas business, but the guidelines ask for proof of 51% woman ownership and every published awardee has been a woman veteran. A 10% owner match is required.
How to apply
Apply online through Submittable from the TWU Veteran Grant page during the application window. Submit formation documents, a Texas Veterans Commission document showing 51% or more ownership, a DD-214, and the CWE company background and project template with a budget showing exactly $5,000 in grant funds.
