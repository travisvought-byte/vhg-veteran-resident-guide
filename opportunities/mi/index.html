Children of Veterans Tuition Grant

Scholarships and school · MI Student Aid (Michigan Department of Treasury) · Anywhere in Michigan

Up to $2,800 a year ($1,400 a semester full-time) toward undergraduate tuition, for up to four years or $11,200, for children of Michigan veterans who died or became totally and permanently disabled from service, or are missing in action.

Who can apply
Parent was a Michigan resident before entering service (or became one after) and was killed in action or died during a war or war condition, died or became totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected condition (per VA), or is MIA in a foreign country. Student is the natural or adopted child, older than 16 and younger than 26, an undergraduate at least half-time at a participating Michigan college, with a 2.25 cumulative GPA, 12 months of Michigan residency, a high school diploma or equivalent, and a FAFSA filed each year.
How to apply
Apply through the MiSSG Student Portal linked from the program page and upload your birth certificate (naming the veteran), the veteran's DD-214, and proof of the veteran's death or total and permanent disability. File the FAFSA every year.
Deadline or date
2026-27 school year: June 30, 2027; 2027-28 school year: June 30, 2028
Where
Participating Michigan public and private nonprofit colleges
Cost
Free
Phone
888-447-2687

Michigan Veterans Trust Fund Emergency Grant Program

Grants and money · Michigan Veterans Trust Fund (MVAA) · Anywhere in Michigan

Discretionary grants for a temporary or short-term financial emergency or hardship; the fund has paid utility bills, home repairs and mortgage help. A 65+ Peacetime Program covers older peacetime-era veterans.

Who can apply
Honorable or general (under honorable conditions) discharge, plus 180 days of active duty during a period of war (or with an Armed Forces or Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal), or at least one day of wartime service with separation for a line-of-duty disability. Peacetime-era veterans with 180 days of service can apply any time during the year they turn 65. Dependents of an eligible veteran may apply in some cases.
How to apply
Apply through the MVTF county committee for the county where you live (county guide on the MVTF page) or with help from a local accredited Veteran Service Officer. Bring your DD-214, proof of residence, the bills you need help with, any disability rating, and proof of income.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time (65+ Peacetime Program: during the year you turn 65)
Where
Statewide in Michigan
Cost
Free
Phone
800-642-4838

Veterans' preference for State of Michigan civil service jobs

Jobs and training · Michigan Civil Service Commission · Anywhere in Michigan

Eligible veterans and certain spouses get preference when applying for initial State of Michigan civil service jobs.

Who can apply
Veteran with 90+ days of active duty and an honorable discharge (or within six months of an expected honorable discharge), a disabled veteran eligible for VA service-connected compensation, a surviving spouse of a veteran, a spouse of a veteran rated more than 50% service-connected, or a surviving spouse with a child under 18 or caring for a disabled child. How to apply
Register with the Michigan Civil Service Commission when applying for state jobs and document your eligibility (DD-214; VA letter for disability).
