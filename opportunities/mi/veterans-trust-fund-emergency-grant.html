Michigan Veterans Trust Fund Emergency Grant Program

Michigan Veterans Trust Fund (MVAA) · Anywhere in Michigan

Grants and money · Michigan Veterans Trust Fund (MVAA) · Anywhere in Michigan

Discretionary grants for a temporary or short-term financial emergency or hardship; the fund has paid utility bills, home repairs and mortgage help. A 65+ Peacetime Program covers older peacetime-era veterans.

Who can apply
Honorable or general (under honorable conditions) discharge, plus 180 days of active duty during a period of war (or with an Armed Forces or Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal), or at least one day of wartime service with separation for a line-of-duty disability. Peacetime-era veterans with 180 days of service can apply any time during the year they turn 65. Dependents of an eligible veteran may apply in some cases.
How to apply
Apply through the MVTF county committee for the county where you live (county guide on the MVTF page) or with help from a local accredited Veteran Service Officer. Bring your DD-214, proof of residence, the bills you need help with, any disability rating, and proof of income.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time (65+ Peacetime Program: during the year you turn 65)
Where
Statewide in Michigan
Cost
Free
Phone
800-642-4838

How many it serves and the odds

  • Serves: 557 emergency grants approved (517 in full, 40 in part), $2,017,443 paid, in FY2025
  • Applicants: 911 applications in FY2025 (156 disapproved, 19 ineligible, 182 withdrawn)
  • Odds: 557 of 911 applications were approved in full or in part in FY2025: about 61%. 182 were withdrawn. (our arithmetic from the program’s own figures)

Figures from michigan.gov, FY2025 (Oct 1, 2024 to Sep 30, 2025).

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
