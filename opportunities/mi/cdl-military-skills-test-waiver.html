CDL skills test waiver for military drivers

Michigan Department of State (Secretary of State) · Anywhere in Michigan

Jobs and training · Michigan Department of State (Secretary of State) · Anywhere in Michigan

Waives certain commercial driver's license tests for service members and veterans based on their military commercial-vehicle training and experience.

Who can apply
Service members and veterans with qualifying military truck or heavy-vehicle driving experience, as set out on the state's military waiver application.
How to apply
Complete the Michigan Application for Military Waiver / Even Exchange (PDF) and submit it as the form directs.
Deadline or date
Not published
Where
Statewide in Michigan
Cost
Free
