CDL testing waivers for military drivers and recent veterans

Jobs and training · Ohio Bureau of Motor Vehicles · Anywhere in Ohio

Service members and veterans separated within 12 months who drove a truck-equivalent vehicle in the military can skip the CDL knowledge test (Military Even Exchange) and/or the skills test (Military Skills Test Waiver).

Who can apply
On active duty or separated within the past 12 months, and in the last 12 months held a military job operating a vehicle equivalent to a commercial motor vehicle (for example Army 88M, 92F, 14T; Air Force 2T1, 2F0, 3E2; Marine Corps 3531; Navy EO). Skills waiver also requires a valid Ohio driver license. Initial CDL only.
How to apply
Bring form BMV 2071 (knowledge test) and/or BMV 2070 (skills test), your license, two-year driving record, DD-214 or military ID, Operator Qualification Record and Military Motor Vehicle Operator's ID card to a driver exam station.
Deadline or date
Within 12 months of separation
Where
Ohio BMV driver exam stations
Cost
Regular CDL fees apply

County veterans burial assistance (ORC 5901.25)

Families and survivors · County Veterans Service Commissions (all 88 Ohio counties) · Anywhere in Ohio

When a veteran, or a veteran's parent, spouse or surviving spouse, dies without the means to pay for the funeral or cremation, the county veterans service commission contracts with a funeral director the family picks to provide burial or cremation at a fair and reasonable price.

Who can apply
The deceased was a veteran, or the parent, spouse or surviving spouse of a veteran, and died without the means to pay necessary funeral or cremation costs. Burial can be in any cemetery in Ohio except those used only for paupers and criminals.
How to apply
Family or friends apply to the veterans service commission in the county and choose the funeral director; the commission contracts with that director. Contact your County Veterans Service Office before arrangements are final.

Every Ohio county has a veterans service commission, funded by a county levy, that gives temporary emergency financial help to veterans and their families who are in need. Each county commission decides what it pays for and how much.

Who can apply
Veterans, active-duty service members, and their spouses, surviving spouses, dependent parents, minor children or wards, who have lived in the county at least three months (ORC 5901.08). Grants and money · Ohio Department of Veterans Services · Anywhere in Ohio

A tax-exempt $500 grant for Ohio service members and veterans injured in combat, or diagnosed with post-traumatic stress, while serving after October 7, 2001. Eligible people can receive one grant per Ohio fiscal year for up to three fiscal years, first come, first served while fund money lasts.

Who can apply
Ohio resident at the time of application, injured in active service after October 7, 2001, proven by a Purple Heart, a combat-related injury with a Combat Action Ribbon or Combat Action Badge, or another combat-related injury such as traumatic brain injury or an IED blast injury; or diagnosed with PTSD while serving after October 7, 2001 (medical diagnosis required).

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
