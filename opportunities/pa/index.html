Amputee and Paralyzed Veterans Pension

Benefits · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

A state pension of $150 a month for veterans who lost, or lost the use of, two or more arms, hands, legs or feet from a service-connected injury or disease.

Who can apply
Pennsylvania resident when entering the military; Honorable or Under Honorable Conditions discharge; service-connected loss or loss of use of two or more extremities, with a VA compensation rating of 40% or higher in each limb.
How to apply
Apply through the County Director of Veterans Affairs in your county. Bring your last DD-214, proof of Pennsylvania residency when you entered the military, and your VA decision letter.

Disabled Veterans' Real Estate Tax Exemption

Benefits · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Exempts the home of a qualifying 100% disabled wartime veteran (or unmarried surviving spouse) from real estate taxes.

Who can apply
Pennsylvania resident; Honorable or Under Honorable Conditions discharge; served during a VA-defined wartime period (an operation or expeditionary medal also counts); VA rating of 100% permanent service-connected, total disability individual unemployability (TDIU), or service-connected blindness, paraplegia or loss of two or more limbs; the home is the principal dwelling, owned solely, jointly with a spouse, or as tenants by the entirety; and financial need. Annual income of $114,637 or less is presumed to show need (effective Jan 1, 2025); above that, need is shown when allowable monthly expenses exceed household income.
How to apply
Apply through the County Director of Veterans Affairs in the county where you live. Veterans previously denied for financial need may reapply under the higher income level.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time
Where
Statewide in Pennsylvania
Cost
Free
Phone
800-547-2838

Educational Gratuity Program

Scholarships and school · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Pays up to $500 per term or semester, sent directly to the school, for up to eight terms (four school years) for children of veterans who are 100% permanently and totally disabled from service or who died in service during a war or armed conflict.

Who can apply
Parent died in service during a period of war or armed conflict, or is a veteran with an Honorable or Under Honorable Conditions discharge who served during VA-defined wartime (or holds an Armed Forces, Navy or Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal) and has a 100% permanent and total service-connected VA rating. Child must be 16 to 23, have lived in Pennsylvania for the five years before applying, attend an approved Pennsylvania school, and show financial need.
How to apply
Apply through the County Director of Veterans Affairs in your county. Bring the veteran's last DD-214 or military death certificate, the student's birth certificate, photo ID, and proof of five years of Pennsylvania residency. The school must send proof of enrollment.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time
Where
Statewide in Pennsylvania
Cost
Free
Phone
800-547-2838

Free vehicle registration for eligible veterans

Benefits · Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Registration and title fees are waived on one passenger vehicle or truck of 9,000 pounds or less for veterans who lost a limb or eye or became partially paralyzed in service, former POWs, Medal of Honor recipients and Purple Heart recipients.

Who can apply
Veterans who lost a limb or an eye or became partially paralyzed while serving in the U.S. Armed Forces; former members captured by the enemy in an armed conflict with a DoD campaign medal; Medal of Honor recipients; Purple Heart recipients. Full criteria are on form MV-371DV.
How to apply
Complete form MV-371DV with the documents it lists and send it to PennDOT as the form directs.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time
Where
Statewide in Pennsylvania
Cost
Free registration and title; license plate fees still apply

Jobs and training · Pennsylvania Department of Transportation (PennDOT) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Lets Pennsylvania service members and recently discharged veterans with military truck experience skip the CDL knowledge and/or skills test.

Military Family Relief Assistance Program (MFRAP)

Grants and money · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Need-based emergency grants of up to $5,000 in a 12-month period that do not have to be repaid, for Pennsylvania service members, recently medically discharged members and their families facing a sudden financial need beyond their control.

Who can apply
Pennsylvania residents serving 30+ consecutive days of active duty (not training) in the armed forces or a reserve component, including the PA National Guard; PA Guard members on 30+ days of state active duty; reserve members in good standing for three years after a qualifying tour; or former members for four years after a medical discharge for a service-incurred disability. Dependent spouses and children can apply, and a spouse, parent, sibling or child can apply to visit a hospitalized service member.
How to apply
Fill out the October 2026 MFRAP application (OVA-BVP-032), then hand it to your county veterans affairs office, mail it to DMVA, Lickdale Veterans' Outreach Center (BVP-MFRAP), 40 Fisher Avenue, Jonestown, PA 17038-8202, or fax it to 717-861-9084, attention MFRAP. Do not email applications.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time
Where
Statewide in Pennsylvania
Cost
Free
Phone
717-861-8910

Pennsylvania National Guard Survivor's Pension

Families and survivors · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Monthly state pension for the family of a Pennsylvania National Guard member killed in the line of duty on state active duty: $100 to $300 a month for a surviving spouse depending on children, payments for minor children if there is no spouse, or $100 for dependent parents.

Who can apply
Survivors of a PA Army or Air National Guard member who was killed or died of line-of-duty injuries on state active duty under the Governor's orders (including armory assemblies and training flights). Not for deaths in federal service, in war or under the President's orders. Spouse is paid up to 10 years or until remarriage; dependent parents up to 10 years; minor children until 18.
How to apply
Fill out the October 2026 application (OVA-BVP-013) and submit it through your county veterans affairs office, by mail to DMVA, Lickdale Veterans' Outreach Center (BVP-Pensions), 40 Fisher Avenue, Jonestown, PA 17038-8202, or by fax to 717-861-9084, attention Pensions. Include ID, marriage or birth certificates, the death certificate and the line-of-duty determination.
Deadline or date
Rolling, apply any time
Where
Statewide in Pennsylvania
Cost
Free
Phone
800-547-2838

Apply or learn more about Pennsylvania National Guard Survivor's Pension on pa.gov

Veterans Temporary Assistance (VTA)

Grants and money · Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) · Anywhere in Pennsylvania

Temporary financial help of up to $1,600 in a 12-month period for the necessities of life: food, shelter, fuel and clothing.

Who can apply
Bona fide Pennsylvania resident who is a veteran discharged under honorable conditions after active duty (not active duty for training), a veteran with a VA-certified service-connected disability, or the unmarried surviving spouse of an eligible veteran (including one who died in service). Must have a temporary shortfall caused by something beyond your control (illness, disability, unemployment, disaster) and not enough aid from other state or federal programs. Receiving food stamps does not disqualify you.
How to apply
Apply through the County Director of Veterans Affairs in the county where you live. Bring your DD-214, proof of Pennsylvania residency, proof of income and expenses, and current mortgage, rent and utility receipts.
