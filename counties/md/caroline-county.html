Veterans help in Caroline County, Maryland

The veterans service office for Caroline County helps veterans, survivors and families apply for the benefits they have earned, at no charge.

DVMF Service and Benefits Program (statewide line)

800-446-4926 ext. 6450

31 Hopkins Plaza, Room 3020, Baltimore, MD 21201

Hours: Monday–Friday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m., by appointment only

No DVMF service office is located in Caroline County, and the department’s list prints no county coverage. Call the statewide service line, which is answered by the Baltimore regional office, and ask which office serves Caroline County. Nearby offices are listed here (Talbot and Wicomico Counties). Department benefits specialists can help with VA claims, pension and survivor benefits. Offices are open by appointment only; call the office first.

Source: Maryland Department of Veterans and Military Families: How To Apply For Benefits (service offices). Checked November 15, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

What the office can help with

  • Filing a VA disability compensation claim, or an increase, and gathering the records it needs
  • VA pension, including Aid and Attendance for veterans and survivors who need help with daily care
  • Survivor benefits, burial and memorial benefits
  • Getting military records, including a copy of the DD214
  • Signing up for VA health care and finding local veterans programs

Call ahead to confirm hours and what to bring. A veterans service office will never charge you to file a VA claim.

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
