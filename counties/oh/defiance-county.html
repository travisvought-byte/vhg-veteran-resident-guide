Veterans help in Defiance County, Ohio

The veterans service office for Defiance County helps veterans, survivors and families apply for the benefits they have earned, at no charge.

Defiance County veterans service office

419-782-6861

Source: Ohio AMVETS 2025–2026 Guidebook. Checked October 8, 2026. Published numbers are not telephone-confirmed.

What the office can help with

  • Filing a VA disability compensation claim, or an increase, and gathering the records it needs
  • VA pension, including Aid and Attendance for veterans and survivors who need help with daily care
  • Survivor benefits, burial and memorial benefits
  • Getting military records, including a copy of the DD214
  • Signing up for VA health care and finding local veterans programs

Call ahead to confirm hours and what to bring. A veterans service office will never charge you to file a VA claim.

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
