# R-04 — Landscape validation: does a facility-facing veteran guide already exist in Ohio?

**Priority:** High. Determines whether the project should build, adapt or simply link.

## Objective
Test the project's central assumption: no Ohio resource currently helps long-term care facility staff identify veteran and surviving-spouse residents and route them to county offices.

## Search targets
1. Ohio Department of Veterans Services outreach or materials for nursing homes, assisted living or long-term care staff.
2. Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman and the regional ombudsman programs covering the four counties.
3. Ohio District 5 Area Agency on Aging and Central Ohio Area Agency on Aging veteran programs or facility materials.
4. VA Central Ohio Healthcare System (and any other VA system serving the four counties) outreach to community nursing homes; VA Community Nursing Home coordinators; Veteran-Directed Care.
5. Ohio Health Care Association, LeadingAge Ohio, or Ohio Assisted Living Association materials on veteran residents.
6. County Veterans Service Commission outreach to facilities in the four counties.
7. National programs active in Ohio facilities (e.g., hospice veteran recognition programs).
8. Models in other states (e.g., Missouri ombudsman veteran/spouse benefits packet) for comparison only.

## Output
`notes.md` with a table: resource, publisher, audience, coverage of the four counties, what it does, and classification as `covered_link_only`, `partial_overlap_adapt`, or `not_relevant`. Close with a one-paragraph assessment of the remaining gap, or a statement that the gap is closed.

## Acceptance checks
- Each entry cites an official or publisher source.
- The assessment distinguishes "not found" from "does not exist."
- Any existing resource that already does this job is flagged at the top.
