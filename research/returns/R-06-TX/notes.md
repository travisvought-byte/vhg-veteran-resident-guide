## Sources and evidence standard

The official Texas Comptroller county list and Texas.gov count establish 254 counties. County names are copied from that list; all entries are county type. The veterans file uses 128 county-government page routes (including full web-page review where a shell fetch was blocked), 115 routes from TexVet’s published county table, and 11 explicitly labeled statewide TVC fallbacks. This is a source hierarchy, not a claim that every county operates its own VSO office.

TexVet identifies itself as a Texas A&M Health Science Center program and the state clearinghouse; the Governor’s official veterans page directly refers residents to its county table. It is an official state-university publication, not an unsupported commercial directory. Its rows remain `published_directory`, with no inferred publication date or telephone confirmation. When a current county page publishes a different phone, that current county route is proposed and both published values are recorded below. Repeated identical phones in staff rows are deduplicated. Distinct alternatives are retained where established.

All JSON source-evidence entries identify URL, supported fields, date reviewed, publication/revision period or null, and access method. Full page/PDF review means the document was retrieved and its relevant contents inspected; it is not direct agency confirmation. Secondary official search extracts are labeled as such inside source evidence. They do not silently become full-page evidence. HICAP is supported by a full CMS program entry; OIB is supported by a full Hockley County government resource guide and corroborated by the indexed state brochure. The Hockley file is labeled 2025 in its filename; its body does not establish a publication date. Failed fetches are not closures.
