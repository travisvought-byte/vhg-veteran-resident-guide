# Texas expansion — wave 01: actionable accessibility funding routes

Reviewed: 2026-10-20. Research-only supplement; **not yet integrated into public cards**. Official published sources; no agencies contacted.

## 1. Texas Veterans Commission — Fund for Veterans' Assistance (FVA) grant services directory
- Official directory: https://tvc.texas.gov/fund
- Official October 5, 2026 announcement: https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-applications-open-for-31-million-in-fund-for-veterans-assistance-grants
- Official June 2, 2026 award announcement: https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-announces-40.8-million-in-grants-to-support-texas-veterans-and-their-families
- **User action:** Select the veteran's county in TVC's grant services directory and identify a currently funded provider offering home modification/repair, assistive technology, transportation, financial assistance, elder care or housing support. Contact that provider for its current intake, geographic service area and documentation.
- **Important distinction:** TVC awards grants to organizations; this is **not** an individual application for a state grant. The 2026–27 award announcement describes 190 grants to 160 organizations; it does not establish individual availability.
- **Proposed card:** `tx-fva-provider-directory`, needs: `equipment_home_accessibility`, `benefits_and_funding`, `housing`, `help_at_home`. Keep statewide with county-filter instructions until individual grantee records are extracted.

## 2. Housing for Texas Heroes (H4TXH)
- Official TVC program appendix (2024–25 period; **historical, not proof of current criteria**): https://tvc.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Appendix-2-to-Annex-B_2024-25-Housing-for-Texas-Heroes.pdf
- Describes home accessibility modification, hazardous-condition repair and weatherization projects administered through funded grantees.
- **User action:** Use the current TVC grant services directory to find a local H4TXH provider; ask if accepting applicants, which counties are covered, whether the veteran's housing situation qualifies, and which documents are required.
- **Caution:** Do not present the historical $35,000 project maximum or income thresholds as current 2026 eligibility without verifying the applicable current grant period.

## 3. Amy Young Barrier Removal Program (AYBR) — non-veteran-specific accessibility
- Official Texas Register funding notice: https://www.sos.texas.gov/texreg/archive/October242025/In%20Addition/In%20Addition.html
- The 2025 notice describes grants for qualifying low-income households with a person with a disability, administered by participating entities, for accessibility modifications and hazard correction.
- **User action:** Check the current TDHCA program and administrator information at https://www.tdhca.texas.gov/notices-funding-availability-nofas ; ask which administrator serves the county and whether household applications are open.
- **Caution:** The notice's $22,500 figure is period-specific and must not be advertised as a current award limit without a fresh official program page.

## 4. Veterans Land Board home improvement loans — hold for status confirmation
- Official August 21, 2026 proposed rules: https://www.sos.texas.gov/texreg/archive/August212026/Proposed%20Rules/40.SOCIAL%20SERVICES%20AND%20ASSISTANCE.html
- VLB voted July 28, 2026 to discontinue offering Veterans Home Improvement Program loans, and proposed removal of program references from regulations.
- **Action:** Flag any legacy VHIP recommendation or archived eligibility page for a live status check before displaying an application instruction. Do not route residents to apply for a loan that may have been discontinued.
- **Open verification:** Check final adopted rule and current VLB product page; the proposed rule alone does not establish final effective date.
