## County boundaries and list validation

- Indiana's official Counties Directory dropdown lists **92 counties**. Its complete county-name set was compared with the 92 DVA county panels and the return. All match; there are no independent-city equivalents in this return. Names retain official directory forms including DeKalb, LaGrange, LaPorte and St. Joseph. Display order is alphabetical, case-insensitive.
- Veterans: DVA publishes a county officer/office contact for **every county**. The return uses 92 county contacts and no substituted statewide entries. Office numbers are selected ahead of fax or separately labeled mobile lines. The Marion entry is the Indianapolis/Marion consolidated government office; the county remains Marion. Marion's published vanity number 317-327-VETS (8387) is normalized to 317-327-8387. LaPorte has a satellite office, not a county split: main extension 2216 is retained; satellite extension 7109 is noted. Miami lists extensions 1860 and 1271; the first is selected and the second noted. Hamilton lists two office numbers; both are preserved with the first as primary.
- Aging: **15 agencies serve 16 planning/service areas**. LifeStream Areas 6 and 9 are one agency record with both county lists, the common published toll-free number and both local office numbers. Every county has exactly one aging agency; no county split was found. The official aging directory, rather than an older SHIP cross-directory, supplies agency names, phones and mappings.
- Ombudsman: **18 referral records** retain the published offices and subdivisions. Areas 10 and 11 share the combined legend office. Lawrence has its own published Area 15 route; Crawford, Orange and Washington use another. Vanderburgh uses VOICES, separately from the other Area 16 counties. Area 12's published number is the statewide ombudsman line, explicitly labeled as such in the record.
- **Marion is geographically split for ombudsman assignment:** NE, SW, E and SE use the common 317-631-9424 office line; NW uses 463-237-9348, also published for Boone. Both records list Marion for county filtering, with explicit subcounty constraints. These overlapping county tags do not claim that both offices serve every part of Marion. The source supplies compass labels, not exact street boundaries; route unclear locations to the statewide office.
- **Randolph has an unresolved ombudsman assignment conflict:** its county panel and Area 6 legend place it in Area 6, while the Area 9 legend also lists Randolph. Both mapped records retain it and flag the overlap. This is a source conflict, not a verified intentional split.
- No county is named Indiana. Ohio is a real Indiana county and must remain county-limited; use only `statewide` for statewide scope.
- No cross-state aging/ombudsman assignment or military-installation service-area override was found in the reviewed referral directories. These directories do not establish comprehensive tribal service boundaries. The PathWays FAQ separately notes tribal enrollment exceptions/opt-in rules; county routing must not be treated as an eligibility determination for tribal members.
