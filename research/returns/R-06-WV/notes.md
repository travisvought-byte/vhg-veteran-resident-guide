## County boundaries and routing

The Legislature lists 55 counties; every one appears exactly once, alphabetically, in the veterans file. There are no independent cities in this list and no county named West Virginia. Ohio is a West Virginia county and must remain a county-scoped entry, not be treated as the Ohio state code.

The veterans department publishes state benefits offices, not a separate county office for each county. Thirteen field offices have usable county assignments covering 53 distinct counties. Cabell and Wayne use the published state office line, 304-558-3661, because the current Huntington claims page does not assign counties. Do not infer a county assignment from the office’s location. Fayette is published under Beckley and Charleston; Monroe under Lewisburg and Princeton. Both options are preserved in the affected county records. The first directory office is the proposed primary; no exclusive boundary is asserted.

The four aging agencies cover all 55 counties, each once: Northwestern 16, Metro 11, Upper Potomac 15 and Appalachian 13. County mappings and phones come from the final 2025–2029 state plan, Attachment D, printed pages 71–72 (PDF pages 76–77). They differ substantially from veterans and ombudsman territories. No reviewed mapping assigns a county to an out-of-state agency or specifies a military-installation or tribal exception. This is not an independent determination of installation or tribal jurisdiction.

Seven indexed regional ombudsman lists cover 54 distinct counties. Kanawha appears as “part” under both Huntington and Charleston, with no city, ZIP or facility boundary provided. Both records preserve this limitation; identify the facility through the statewide route. Doddridge is absent from all seven lists. It has the statewide ombudsman route at 800-834-0598; no regional assignment was invented. Legal Aid’s general legal-office counties are not ombudsman territories and must not replace this map.

Regional ombudsman numbers and extensions are recorded by the published office/advocate role, without staff names or personal details. They are not asserted to be general legal-services intake lines. The statewide ombudsman number is distinct from Legal Aid’s general legal helpline.
