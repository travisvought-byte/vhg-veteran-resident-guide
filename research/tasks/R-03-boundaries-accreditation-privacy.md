# R-03 — Operating boundaries: claims accreditation, fee rules, privacy, and scams

**Priority:** Critical path. A draft boundary statement must exist before any guide is drafted.

## Objective
Establish, from official sources, what facility staff, VHG staff and volunteers may and may not do when helping a veteran or surviving spouse with benefits, and draft a short boundary statement for review by an attorney or qualified advisor.

## Questions
1. Federal rules on who may assist in the preparation, presentation or prosecution of VA claims, and the accreditation categories (VSO representatives, attorneys, claims agents). Cite the regulation and VA's accreditation pages.
2. What unaccredited people may do without violating those rules (e.g., giving general information, referring to an accredited representative). State only what official sources support.
3. Fee rules: when fees may and may not be charged for VA benefits assistance.
4. Official warnings about "pension poaching" or fee-charging benefit advisers targeting older veterans (VA, FTC, Ohio Attorney General, Ohio DVS, Long-Term Care Ombudsman).
5. Privacy: what general constraints apply when a long-term care facility shares resident information with an outside organization. Identify official guidance only; do not provide legal conclusions about VHG's specific status.
6. Whether Ohio law or rule requires nursing homes to ask about or record veteran status, or to provide county office information to veterans. If none is found, say so. Note comparable laws in other states only as examples.
7. Any Ohio Long-Term Care Ombudsman material on veterans in long-term care.

## Output
`notes.md` with findings and citations; `boundary_statement_draft.md` (under 300 words, plain language, marked **DRAFT — requires attorney or qualified advisor review**); `scam_warning_draft.md` (under 120 words).

## Acceptance checks
- Every rule cited to a regulation, statute or official agency page.
- Drafts contain no legal conclusions about VHG's own obligations; those are listed as questions for the reviewer.
