Ohio Bingo Finder

Instant and electronic bingo at Ohio posts and lodges

Ohio has three kinds of charitable bingo. This page explains them and lists the 946 veterans posts, fraternal lodges and clubs licensed to sell instant bingo to their members and guests.

Find a bingo game

Bingo games

Numbers called in a hall, played on paper or card-minders. Most games are open to the public. Find them in the bingo finder.

Instant bingo tickets

Paper pull-tabs with a set prize structure. Sold during bingo games, and at some posts and lodges outside games.

Electronic instant bingo

The same game on a terminal. Only veterans and fraternal organizations may offer it. 912 locations below are licensed for it.

Who can play here

Outside of bingo games, these posts and lodges may sell instant bingo only on their own premises, to their own members and invited guests, for up to 12 hours a day between 10 a.m. and 2 a.m. (ORC 2915.13; electronic games also follow ORC 2915.14). Players must be 18 or older.

This is not a list of places open to the public. If you are a veteran or want to join a post or lodge, call or visit and ask how to become a member. Many posts welcome new members and their families.

Charities also sell paper instant bingo tickets through bars and other businesses. Those locations are not listed here.

Proposed changes: House Bill 344 would let 501(c)(3) charities run electronic instant bingo at bars and restaurants and allow posts and lodges to sell up to 16 hours a day starting at 8 a.m. As of November 12, 2026, it remains in the House Finance Committee and is not law (bill page). The 2025 state budget left the bingo hours and distribution rules unchanged.

Licensed posts and lodges

Community partner halls

Posts and lodges must give part of their instant bingo net profit to a qualifying charity. Halls that name Veteran Home Guardians as a recipient, for any share, are Community Partners and are marked on this list and in the bingo finder. Every listing is free either way.
