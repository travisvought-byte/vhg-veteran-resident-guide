Ohio Bingo Finder

Partner with the Ohio Bingo Finder

A free statewide finder that helps seniors, veterans, families and activity directors find bingo games and senior centers near them. It is built and kept up by Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio nonprofit that visits veterans in nursing homes.

Run a bingo game?

American Legion and VFW posts, booster clubs, churches, fire departments and other licensed charities: get your game in front of players and the activity staff who plan resident outings.

Send your game days, door and first-game times, packet prices, a public phone or Facebook page, and whether the entrance is step-free and groups are welcome.

Nursing homes and senior living

Activity directors and social workers can print a one-page list of games and senior centers near their building, with a QR code to the full finder. It works well on activity boards, in family packets and in resident newsletters.

Senior centers, libraries and churches

Hang the poster, link to the finder from your website or newsletter, and send us your own program schedule so it shows up for people searching nearby.

Sponsors and community partners

Partners help keep schedules current, reach every bingo hall and care facility in the state, and get printed flyers into buildings where residents don't use the internet. Support goes to Veteran Home Guardians' work with veterans in Ohio nursing homes.
