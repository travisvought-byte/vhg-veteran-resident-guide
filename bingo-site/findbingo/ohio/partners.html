Ohio Bingo Finder

Partner with the Ohio Bingo Finder

A free statewide finder that helps seniors, veterans, families and activity directors find bingo games and senior centers near them. It is built and kept up by Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio nonprofit that visits veterans in nursing homes.

Open the finder

Listings are free and always will be. Inclusion never depends on donations, partnership, sponsorship or advertising.

Run a bingo game?

American Legion and VFW posts, booster clubs, churches, fire departments and other licensed charities: get your game in front of players and the activity staff who plan resident outings.

Send your game days, door and first-game times, packet prices, a public phone or Facebook page, and whether the entrance is step-free and groups are welcome.

Get verified, free. When someone from your hall confirms your days and times, your listing shows a “Verified” badge with the month you confirmed. It stays for 12 months; confirm again each year to keep it.

Nursing homes and senior living

Activity directors and social workers can print a one-page list of games and senior centers near their building, with a QR code to the full finder. It works well on activity boards, in family packets and in resident newsletters.

Community partner halls

Instant bingo halls run by veteran's, fraternal and sporting organizations must give part of their net profit to a qualifying charity. Halls that name Veteran Home Guardians as one of their recipients become Community Partners.

We ask for a modest share, usually 5 to 10 percent of what a hall gives, not all of it. At minimum, it should cover visits to veterans in nursing homes in your county. Anything beyond that helps us reach counties that have no local support. Every hall is different, so we work it out together.

Partners are recognized on this page and on their listing. We help partners stay current on the distribution rules, send updates on our work, and visit senior veterans in nursing homes in their area.

No hall can pay for a better listing. Every hall gets the same free listing whether it partners with us or not.

Would rather not partner? We can feature a special bingo event for $100, build and keep up a website for your hall for a fee, or help with advertising. Ways to work with us
