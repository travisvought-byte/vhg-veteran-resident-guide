Run a bingo game in Michigan? Get it listed free

Send us the name of your hall, the address, the days and the time the first game starts. A phone number for players helps too. Listing is free, and we never sell or share your information.

Email your game times

What Michigan law says about your bingo money

The entire net proceeds of a bingo event must go to the licensed group’s own lawful purposes.

Advertising your game in Michigan

Where the state lets a group advertise bingo, the ad must say what the net proceeds will be used for.

Checked November 20, 2026. Sources: MCL 432.109 ↗ · MCL 432.110 ↗. This is a summary, not legal advice; your regulator has the final word.

In Michigan that money stays with your group for its own causes, so we never ask for a share of it. What we offer is help filling seats:

  • Your listing is free, always. No hall can pay for a better one.
  • Help getting the word out. Free tips, and paid advertising on Find Bingo if you want more.
  • Featured events. Put a special bingo night in its own box for people searching near you. How featured events work
  • A game site for your hall. We build it, host it and keep it current from the updates you send us, for one flat monthly fee. You get a page to put on flyers, signs and social media. Details
