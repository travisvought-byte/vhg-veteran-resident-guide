Privacy

Wondering what we keep about you? Very little: there is no account or sign-in, visits are counted without cookies, and your ZIP code search is worked out on your own device. Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name.
  • We count visits without cookies and without keeping personal information.
  • Your ZIP code search is worked out on your own phone or computer.
  • The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which sees your internet address and the area on the map.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Counting visits

We use GoatCounter to count page views, so we know which pages people use. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store personal information. For each page view it records things like the page visited, the site that sent you here, your browser, your screen size, and an approximate country worked out from your internet (IP) address. It does not store the IP address.

We leave out everything after the question mark in a page address, so the ZIP code you search for is never counted.

Your ZIP code search

When you search by ZIP code, the page finds bingo near you on your own device. It downloads the bingo lists and a list of ZIP code locations, then works out the distances itself. Ohio’s finder works the same way. The ZIP code is not sent to us or to anyone else to look it up, and we do not keep it.

Your ZIP code does show in the page address (for example, findbingo.org/?zip=48161), so you can bookmark or share a search. If you search from a state page, or open Ohio’s finder with your ZIP code, your browser asks our web host for that address, the same way it asks for any web page.

The map and “Use my location”

The map pictures (tiles) come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). To draw the map, your browser asks OpenStreetMap for the pictures of the area you look at, so OpenStreetMap sees your internet (IP) address, the area on the map and which website you are on, like any website you visit. Your browser does not send it the ZIP code you typed or your location, but the area on the map is near the place you searched. Read the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s privacy policy. The map code itself (Leaflet) comes from this site. If you hide the map, your browser remembers that on your device, and the map pictures stop loading.

“Use my location” asks your browser for your location. If you say yes, the page finds the nearest ZIP code on your own device and searches it like a typed one. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not kept.

Who serves the site

findbingo.org is served by Cloudflare. Some files, such as the bingo lists, can also load from GitHub Pages, where the site’s code is kept. Like any web host, Cloudflare and GitHub may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Advertising

This site may show ads. Every ad is labeled “Advertisement” and kept apart from the game listings. If we use an ad network such as Google AdSense, it and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at adssettings.google.com and learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, or opt out of many networks at optout.aboutads.info.

Ads never change which games are listed or their order. About advertising on Find Bingo

Email, calls and directions

If you email us game times or a correction, we get your email address and what you write. We use it to update the listings and to reply, and we never sell or share it. The call, phone lookup and directions buttons open your phone or Google Maps, which follow their own privacy rules.

Children

Find Bingo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
