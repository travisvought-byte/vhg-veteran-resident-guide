What Missouri law says about your bingo money

The entire net receipts above the actual cost of the game must go to the lawful, charitable, religious or philanthropic purposes of the group running it.

Advertising your game in Missouri

Advertising each bingo occasion is an allowed expense at fair market cost, bought under the Gaming Commission’s rules.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: RSMo 313.040 ↗. This is a summary, not legal advice; your regulator has the final word.
