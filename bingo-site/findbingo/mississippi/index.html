Licensed bingo halls in Mississippi

Looking for a bingo hall in Mississippi? These are the halls licensed for charity bingo on the Mississippi Gaming Commission’s map, in places like Hattiesburg, Biloxi and Columbus. The state doesn’t list game days, so call the hall before you drive over.

Shows bingo near you in every state we cover, closest first.

Mississippi charitable bingo is licensed by the Mississippi Gaming Commission.

Mississippi's map names halls licensed for bingo. It does not show game days or times, so call before you go. Halls whose license on the map has passed its end date are not shown.

What Mississippi law says about your bingo money

All net proceeds from bingo must be spent only on the purposes the charitable organization was created for.

Checked November 21, 2026. Sources: 13 Miss. Code R. 10-5.5 ↗. This is a summary, not legal advice; your regulator has the final word.

In Mississippi that money stays with your group for its own causes, so we never ask for a share of it. What we offer is help filling seats:

  • Your listing is free, always. No hall can pay for a better one.
  • Help getting the word out. Free tips, and paid advertising on Find Bingo if you want more.
  • Featured events. Put a special bingo night in its own box for people searching near you. How featured events work
  • A game site for your hall. We build it, host it and keep it current from the updates you send us, for one flat monthly fee. You get a page to put on flyers, signs and social media. Details
