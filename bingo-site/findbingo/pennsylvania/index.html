Pennsylvania bingo licenses come from each county, so there is no statewide list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. In Pennsylvania, bingo licenses are issued by each county treasurer's office.

Cumberland County

Bingo

American Legion Post 109

Mechanicsburg

Players must be 18 or older. Guests are signed in, and after three visits a guest must join the post.

Bingo days:

  • Sunday, 6 p.m. · during bingo season

The post holds bingo on Sundays during its bingo season and does not post the season dates. Call before you go.
