Advertise on Find Bingo

Reach people looking for bingo near them: players, their families, and the activity staff who plan outings.

Who can advertise

Bingo halls and the groups that run games first of all, then bingo suppliers, local businesses, senior services, senior living and home care, transportation, and community events. If your business is a good fit for bingo players and their families, write to us.

Feature your bingo event: $100

Running a special bingo night, a fundraiser or a holiday game? For $100 we feature it in its own box, labeled “Featured event,” for people who search near your hall and on your state’s page, from the day it goes up until the day of the event.

  • The event must be run by a group allowed to hold bingo where it takes place.
  • A featured event never adds to, removes or reorders the free game listings. Your hall’s weekly games stay listed free either way.
  • Send the event name, your hall or group, the address, date and start time, a short description (up to 200 characters), and a link or phone number. We confirm the details and send an invoice.

A game site for your hall: $100 a month

Want a website for your games that you can put on flyers, signs, Facebook and billboards? We build it for you. After that, send us your updates by email or text (new times, specials, holiday closings, flyers) and we post them.

  • One flat fee of $100 a month covers the site, its hosting and the updates you send.
  • Your games stay listed free on Find Bingo whether or not you have a site with us.

For bingo halls

Every hall’s listing is free, always. Beyond that, choose what fits your hall:

  • Community partner (Ohio). In Ohio, veteran, fraternal and sporting groups must give part of their instant bingo profit to an outside charity, and can name Veteran Home Guardians as one of their recipients. We ask for a modest share, usually 5 to 10 percent of what you give, not all of it. At minimum, it should cover visits to veterans in nursing homes in your county. Anything beyond that helps us reach counties that have no local support. How partnership works Other states treat bingo money differently, and we only offer what fits your state’s rules. Ground rules

  • Every ad is labeled “Advertisement.” Every featured event is labeled “Featured event.”
  • Ads sit apart from the game listings: on the home page below the search results, and on state pages after the listings. Never inside them. Featured events sit in their own box above the results, never inside them.
  • Ads never add, remove, change or reorder listings. A hall cannot pay for a better listing, and every listing stays free.
  • We review every ad before it runs, and we turn down ads that are misleading.

Where the money goes

Ad income supports Veteran Home Guardians, the Ohio nonprofit that runs Find Bingo and visits veterans in nursing homes.
