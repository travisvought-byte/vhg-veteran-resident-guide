YOUR WORDS. YOUR CHOICE.

1. Choose your request

No military service is required. You can speak for yourself or describe a concern as a family member, caregiver or neighbor.

Ohio lawmakers handle Ohio laws and state programs. Congress handles federal laws and programs such as Medicare. You can write separate messages to both.

Use only details you want to share. Keep medical records and claim numbers out of the message.

3. Contact your lawmakers

For this Ohio policy request, use your state senator and state representative. Ask the office to explain its role and direct agency questions to the responsible program.

Find your Ohio senator (new tab) · Find your Ohio representative (new tab)
  1. Copy your message, then open the official contact link.
  2. Choose your lawmaker. Paste the subject and message into their form and add the details their office requests.
  3. Review and send on the official site. Keep any confirmation and note when you want to follow up.

You can also use the message for a phone call, relay call or printed letter. Choose the method that works for you.

Contact lookups open official sites. Enter your address there to identify your district. This page does not look up your address, send messages or save your text. Edits last only while the page is open.
