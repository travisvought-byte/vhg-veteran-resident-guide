MILITARY & VETERAN FAMILIES

If your family serves or served

Active duty families have programs through the Defense Department and TRICARE. Veterans' children may qualify for VA health coverage, and a county veterans service officer can help with dependency claims, including for an adult child who has been disabled since childhood.

Disability rights agencies in every state

Every state has a federally mandated protection and advocacy agency that gives free information, advocacy and sometimes legal help to people with disabilities, including about special education. The list comes from the National Disability Rights Network's member list.

    Good to know

    • Ask your public school district for a free evaluation in writing if your child is 3 or older, even if your child goes to a private school or is homeschooled. Keep a copy and note the date.
    • A medical diagnosis and school eligibility are separate decisions. A child can get an IEP or 504 plan without a particular diagnosis.
    • This guide lists free public programs and nonprofits. It does not list private therapy providers, and listing a program does not mean VHG endorses or partners with it.
    • Contacts come from published official sources and were not confirmed by phone. If something is wrong, use "Report a problem" on the listing.
