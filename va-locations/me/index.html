Not enrolled in VA health care?

Call 877-222-8387 or apply with the help of a free veterans service officer: find your county’s veterans office.

Need help now?

Crisis: call 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line, or text 838255. In immediate danger, call 911.

Homeless or about to lose housing: call 877-424-3838, the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, any time, day or night.
