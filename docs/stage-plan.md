# Stage plan

Stages advance on evidence, not dates. One research batch is open per workstream at a time.

| Stage | Work | Completion gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Research foundation | R-01 to R-05 issued and returned | Each return passes its acceptance checks or its gaps are logged |
| 2. Boundaries and content | Boundary statement (R-03) reviewed by an attorney or qualified advisor; benefit topics (R-02) reduced to plain-language routing notes | No sentence in the draft guide determines eligibility or offers claims help |
| 3. Confirmation | Travis or a designee contacts the four county offices (D-013) to confirm contact details, referral preference and visit options | All four county records reach `agency_confirmed` for contact fields, or the gap is shown on the sheet |
| 4. Draft and test | Handoff sheets, staff guide and a minimal searchable page; tested with at least two facility staff and, where they agree, one resident or family member | Testers reach the correct county office without help; confusing parts are fixed or removed |
| 5. Release and maintain | Distribution to pilot facilities with administrator agreement; maintenance owner named (D-014); quarterly recheck scheduled | Every listing shows a last-checked date; a correction route works end to end |

Expansion beyond the four counties is a separate decision after Stage 5.
