# Guide distribution plan

Updated October 8, 2026. Public share kit: `share.html`. It includes a one-page QR flyer, reusable newsletter introduction and direct links to accessibility, seniors and surviving-spouse support. The QR links to the stable guide homepage; county-specific links can be copied from the guide.

## First distribution wave

Start with Centerburg Pointe, Bennington Glen, Morrow Manor and Woodside Village, where VHG already has a visitation program. Ask social services, admissions and discharge staff to place the flyer where families can find it and include the blurb in family communications. Ask one staff member at each location to try a real referral and report whether the next step was clear, without sharing resident information.

Next, offer the relevant county-specific view to veterans offices, aging/disability contacts and community posts in Morrow, Knox, Marion and Delaware. Request newsletter or website inclusion rather than describing a listing as an established partnership. Official post locators are available; individual post contacts still need research.

After that pilot feedback, request wider distribution through Ohio's aging agency network and care-provider associations. Their newsletters and member communications are potential channels; publication or endorsement has not been secured.

## Match the link to the audience

- Facility staff: county contacts and the printable handoff.
- Seniors, caregivers and disability groups: `?view=seniors`.
- Ramp and accessibility requests: `?view=accessibility`.
- Surviving spouses: `?view=survivors`.
- Posts and community groups: `?view=community`.

For Facebook, use the new social preview and add the link in the comment as planned. Existing preview caches may take time to refresh. Keep resource access free and place the optional VHG support appeal after the practical referral.

## Measure usefulness

Keep a private distribution log: organization, public contact role, channel, date requested, date shared, response and next follow-up. Count confirmed placements and feedback about usable referrals, not just impressions. Do not collect resident identities or benefit outcomes in the public repository. No tracking service or outreach campaign was activated by this update.

Next practical research: temporary ramp/reuse programs and local funding gaps, followed by source-reviewed contractor contacts and funder requirements. More listings should follow usable routes and a working maintenance process.
