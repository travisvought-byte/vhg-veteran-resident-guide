# California coverage and source evidence

**Reviewed:** October 19, 2026. **Scope:** initial published-source referral edition, not an inventory of every local program.

California adds all **58 counties**, with one county/cross-county veterans referral, a county-matched aging information line and a route to the long-term-care ombudsman program. It contains 13 California programs and 93 regional referral records: 59 aging county/City information lines (mapping the state's 33 Area Agencies on Aging) and 34 long-term-care ombudsman jurisdiction entries.

## Veterans service offices

The California Association of County Veterans Service Officers [published directory](https://www.cacvso.org/county-contacts/) provides county office telephone listings. The [CalVet statewide number](https://www.ca.gov/departments/163/) is 800-952-5626.

- **Alpine:** No separate county office was found in the association directory; use the statewide CalVet referral until a current local assignment is published.
- **Sierra:** The association lists a county office but leaves its phone blank. The guide shows the statewide CalVet fallback, not an inferred neighboring office phone.
- **Mono:** Uses Inyo-Mono veterans services. **Yuba and Sutter:** Share a Yuba-Sutter office number. **San Benito:** Uses a Monterey regional office contact as published.

The directory is an association source, not a telephone verification of individual office hours, staffing or intake.

## Aging county lines

The [California Department of Aging county contact table](https://aging.ca.gov/Find_Services_in_my_County/) supplies specific phone numbers for all 58 counties, plus a distinct Los Angeles City line. The state contracts with 33 Area Agencies on Aging, so the 59 published helpline entries do not represent 59 separate agencies.

Some planning and service areas span multiple counties with different published intake numbers. Los Angeles County's aging number is 626-414-6439; Los Angeles City's is 213-482-7252. Ask for the correct geographic service area rather than assuming they are interchangeable. Statewide aging and adult information: **800-510-2020**.

## Long-term-care ombudsman jurisdictions

The [California Department of Aging local provider directory](https://aging.ca.gov/Providers_and_Partners/Long-Term_Care_Ombudsman/Providers_List/) maps 34 jurisdiction entries in this edition. Its boundaries are distinct from aging-agency planning areas, notably for San Luis Obispo/Santa Barbara and Napa/Solano.

**The phone shown on local ombudsman entries is the statewide hotline, 800-231-4024, not a verified local office number.** Give the facility's address and request referral to the responsible local advocate. Local direct lines remain a research priority; do not imply those lines were individually verified.

## Medicaid, accessibility and other state referrals

California's HCBA and MSSP home and community services have distinct rules and geographic/eligibility limitations. MSSP lists Environmental Accessibility Adaptations among possible services, but this does not establish a person's enrollment, construction funding or approval. Ask the assigned waiver case manager or local program to document the needs assessment, eligible scope, vendor requirements and written approval before work. The guide also refers visitors to HICAP Medicare counseling, caregiver supports, Ability Tools, Disability Rights California, California Blind Field Services, Relay and participating local 211 programs.

## County office details

All 57 county offices listed in the CACVSO directory now show an address, published hours and the office website. Satellite offices and outreach sites are listed where the directory gives them. Every phone in the guide matched the directory. Source: https://www.cacvso.org/county-contacts/, reviewed 2026-11-02.

- **Shared offices:** Inyo and Mono share the Bishop office. Yuba and Sutter share the Marysville office.
- **Alpine:** not in the directory, so it keeps the statewide CalVet fallback.
- **Sierra:** the directory lists an address but no phone.
- **Details left out where the directory text was inconsistent:**
  - Butte: the address was listed with the wrong city.
  - Los Angeles: the "Long Beach" satellite entry was garbled.
  - Alameda, Temecula and Barstow: mistyped ZIP codes were dropped.

|---|---|

## Independent Living Centers

28 `ca-cil-*` records cover all 58 counties. Phones, websites and counties served come from the ILRU national directory (https://www.ilru.org/cil-directory/ca, reviewed in full). Centers with only http websites use the ILRU page as `official_url` and print the website in the next step.

## Civil and senior legal aid

- **General legal aid:**
  - Legal Services of Northern California: eight office cards.
  - Bay Area Legal Aid, Inland Counties Legal Services and Central California Legal Services: from their own pages, reviewed.
  - Greater Bakersfield Legal Assistance, Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles, Neighborhood Legal Services of Los Angeles County, Community Legal Aid SoCal, Legal Aid Society of San Diego and California Rural Legal Assistance: from search excerpts.
  - LawHelpCA: a statewide card.
- **Senior legal services (60+):** 22 providers from the California Department of Aging providers list, which was reviewed in full. That list prints no phones, so these cards route through the provider or the Area Agency on Aging.
- **Coverage:** every county has a county-specific legal card except Inyo and Mono, which get the statewide LawHelpCA card.

## Veteran housing (SSVF)

County provider cards come from VA's FY26 SSVF provider intake list: 29 grants covering 55 of 58 counties. Alpine, Inyo and Mono rely on the national VA housing line and 211. See [SSVF-HOUSING.md](SSVF-HOUSING.md).
