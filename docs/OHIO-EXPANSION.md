# Ohio program expansion — October 9, 2026

Six source-reviewed records add direct intake routes without adding new interface sections:

- Axess SSVF: nine northeast Ohio counties; housing coverage increases from 30 to 39 counties.
- District 5 caregiver support: nine counties; respite eligibility explicitly distinguishes community residents age 60+ from institutional residents.
- Center for Disability Empowerment: Delaware, Franklin, Licking and Union.
- Southeastern Ohio CIL: nine counties, including Washington on the current provider page (absent from the older SILC directory).
- Advancing Independence Network: Summit, Stark, Portage and Wayne; living skills, advocacy and equipment lending.
- LEAP: seven northeast Ohio counties; benefits and nursing-home transition support.

Each record retains dated source evidence and published contacts. Phones are not telephone-confirmed. County filters apply to all new records; general Ohio independent-living routing remains available outside these areas. Caregiver and disability records appear in home support, care and the relevant senior/accessibility topics. Other states retain their existing resources.

Next priorities: local housing providers in the remaining 39 counties, direct confirmation of independent-living service availability, additional regional respite programs and direct meal/transportation intake routes. Broad office footprints are not treated as program coverage.
