# Washington referral edition

Reviewed November 4, 2026. Washington moves from a starter edition to a full 39-county edition.

## Veterans contacts (`washington-county-veterans-offices.json`)

Each county uses the "County Veterans Assistance Fund or Program" entry on its Washington Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA) county page (`dva.wa.gov/resources/county/<County>`). All 39 pages were read in full.

- **What the cards show.** Phone, address, website and hours come from that entry. A few listed second numbers appear as "Also." Staff names and emails were not published.
- **Counties on the WDVA line.** Adams and Franklin list no county program. Pend Oreille lists a program but no phone. All three use the WDVA statewide line (800-562-2308), with a note saying why.
- **Copied as listed.** Garfield's address on the page lacks a city, and Grays Harbor's lacks an address. Both are kept as published. Clallam uses the Northwest Veterans Resource Center address the page gives for that program.
- **Websites.** Only https county websites are linked.

## Aging and ombuds

- **Aging agencies (11 records, `wa-aging-*`).** Counties served come from the WA Cares Fund Area Agency on Aging contact sheet. Consumer phones come from the DSHS AAA directory and contact lists; King County uses Community Living Connections. Each county is covered by exactly one agency. Every card names the Yakama Nation and Colville Indian Area Agencies on Aging for tribal elders.
- **Ombuds (`wa-ombudsman`).** The statewide Long-Term Care Ombuds Program, 800-562-6028, from DSHS. County cards also give the DSHS facility abuse and neglect hotline, 800-562-6078.

## Other records (`wa-programs.json`)

- **Housing help (SSVF).** The 8 programs now use county lists from VA's FY26 intake workbook.
- **Centers for Independent Living (4 records).** Taken from the ILRU national directory, with full page text reviewed. Center for Independence lists "South King," and its card says so.
- **Legal aid.** From Washington Courts and the Office of Civil Legal Aid:
  - Northwest Justice Project's CLEAR hotline: 38 counties outside King, 888-201-1014.
  - King County: 2-1-1 or 877-211-9274.
  - CLEAR*Sr: statewide for age 60+, 888-387-7111.
- **VA health care.** One statewide card for VA Puget Sound (Seattle and American Lake), Spokane and Walla Walla, noting the VA Portland system for southwest Washington. No counties are assigned.

## Limits

Contacts are published-source referrals and were not telephone-confirmed. Washington waiver (COPES) and home-modification program details are not yet researched.
