# Missouri referral edition

Reviewed November 4, 2026. Missouri moves from a starter edition to a full edition covering all 114 counties and the City of St. Louis (115 localities). St. Louis City is an independent city and is labeled without "County."

## Veterans contacts (`missouri-county-veterans-offices.json`)

Missouri's county veterans help comes from state Veterans Service Officers employed by the Missouri Veterans Commission (MVC). Each locality uses the MVC office whose printed "County Coverage" includes it.

- **Source.** The service office pages of the [MVC Veterans Benefits & Resources Guide](https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/docs/veterans-benefits-guide.pdf), with 32 offices. The guide has no printed date.
- **Phones.** Numbers printed as "573-817-4272/4273" are expanded, and the extra numbers are shown as "Also."
- **Chillicothe.** This office (Carroll, Grundy, Livingston and Mercer) has no printed phone, so the MVC main line, 573-751-3779, is shown with a note.
- **Shared coverage.** Crawford, Dent, Maries and Phelps are covered by both St. James and Rolla. Phelps lists Rolla first; the others list St. James first, with the other office under "Other MVC offices."
- **Fixes to printed text.** The guide prints St. James's ZIP as "6559" (corrected to 65559) and spells DeKalb as "Deklab."
- **Left out.** Staff names and emails.

## Aging and ombudsman (`mo-regional-referrals.json`)

- **Area Agencies on Aging (10, `mo-aging-r*`).** Taken from the DHSS [Area Agencies on Aging table](https://health.mo.gov/partners-providers/silver-haired-legislature/missouri-silver-haired-legislature-delegations).
  - The toll-free number is the main phone; the local number is shown as a note.
  - The table leaves Ripley County out of Region 2. It is added there because other listings for Aging Matters include it.
  - Region 7 is printed as "Mid-Missouri Regional Council." It is the Mid-America Regional Council (MARC), and its local number, 816-474-4240, comes from the DHSS ombudsman map.
  - The Missouri Senior Resource Line, 800-235-5503, is a statewide card.
- **Regional ombudsman programs.** Mapped to the aging regions, from the DHSS ombudsman materials:

  | Program | Aging regions |
  | --- | --- |
  | Council of Churches of the Ozarks | 1 and 10 |
  | Aging Matters | 2 |
  | Care Connection | 3 |
  | Young at Heart | 4 |
  | Aging Best | 6 |
  | MARC | 7 |
  | VOYCE | 8 and 9 |

  No current official phone was found for Northeast Missouri (Region 5), so those counties use the state ombudsman line, 800-309-3282.
- **Abuse reporting.** The Adult Abuse and Neglect Hotline, 800-392-0210, is on every county panel.

## Other records (`mo-programs.json`)

- **Housing help (SSVF).** The 6 programs now use county lists from VA's FY26 intake workbook.
- **Centers for Independent Living (21 records).** From the ILRU directory, with branches folded into their main center.
  - DRA is listed as "Jefferson and surrounding counties" and is assigned to Jefferson only.
  - DCAI's wider consumer-directed services counties are noted but not assigned.
- **Legal aid.** One statewide card for the four legal services programs. No counties are assigned; lsmo.org shows the program for each county.
- **VA health care.** One statewide card for St. Louis, Kansas City, Columbia and Poplar Bluff. No counties are assigned.
