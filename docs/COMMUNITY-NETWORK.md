# County community connections

The initial page provides official locators:

- American Legion: https://mylegion.org/PersonifyEbusiness/Find-a-Post
- AMVETS: https://www.amvets.org/find-a-post
- VFW: https://vfw.org/find-a-post

Individual county records are not yet populated. Add only source-reviewed contacts to `data/public/community-posts.json`, preserving the distinction between a locator and a verified post listing.

Each future post record should include: stable post ID; organization; post number and name; town; physical-location county; any separately confirmed service counties; public organizational phone and email; official website/source URL; date reviewed; evidence status; and services or partnership contact role only if explicitly documented. Do not infer county from the nearest city or assume a post serves only its physical county. Do not copy member lists or private contact information.

Research order: Morrow, Knox, Marion and Delaware, then neighboring counties, then the remaining Ohio counties. Check official department/post directories, compare against each post’s own website, and flag conflicts or inactive listings. A reachable website is not telephone confirmation. Keep posts separate from accredited representatives, government benefits offices and guaranteed funding providers.

Use the directory to identify possible guide distribution, visitation and sponsorship contacts. Track actual outreach and replies in the organization’s private working system rather than the public resource listing. Listing a post does not imply that it is a VHG partner.
