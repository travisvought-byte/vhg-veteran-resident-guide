# Florida referral coverage and source evidence

**Reviewed:** October 19, 2026. **Status:** initial published-source referral edition; not a verified inventory of all local assistance.

Florida has **67 counties** with a county veterans contact, matched aging helpline and county-matched long-term-care ombudsman route. This edition includes 13 statewide programs, 11 Area Agencies on Aging (12 helpline contact entries because Palm Beach has a distinct local phone) and 14 long-term-care ombudsman district listings. It shares national VA and caregiver resources with other states.

## Published source priorities

- County veterans offices: [Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs March 2026 CVSO directory](https://floridavets.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CVSO-Directory-March-2026.pdf). **Collier County** corrected using [Collier's county-owned veterans page](https://www.collier.gov/Resident-Resources/Community-and-Human-Services-Division/Veteran-Services): 239-252-8387; the FDVA PDF incorrectly prints an extra trailing digit.
- Aging and ADRC routes: [Florida Department of Elder Affairs Elder Helpline by county](https://elderaffairs.org/about-us/elder-helpline-1-800-963-5337/), statewide 800-963-5337. The published Palm Beach-specific local helpline is **561-214-8600**; Indian River, Martin, Okeechobee and St. Lucie use 866-684-5885. These are contact entries for the aging network, not twelve independent Area Agencies on Aging.
- [Florida Long-Term Care Ombudsman district directory](https://ombudsman.elderaffairs.org/about-us/district-offices/) lists fourteen district contacts. North Miami-Dade versus South Miami-Dade divides at **Flagler Street**; the North Dade office includes Hialeah and N.E./N.W. addresses and the South Dade/Keys office includes southern Miami-Dade and Monroe. County-only selection must show both contacts with that geographic distinction and statewide routing at 888-831-0404.
- [Florida AHCA's SMMC Long-Term Care program](https://ahca.myflorida.com/medicaid/statewide-medicaid-managed-care/long-term-care-program/find-out-about-long-term-care-services.html) includes **Home Accessibility Adaptation**, which is not a general home-improvement grant. Enrolled members should ask the plan case manager about the person's care plan, authorization and vendors. New applicants should begin with the Elder Helpline.
- FAAST, Florida SHINE, Disability Rights Florida, the Division of Blind Services, Florida Relay and Florida 211 provide the additional statewide referral routes.

## County office details

County veterans office records can carry an address, hours, email, website and county-published walk-in outreach sessions, shown on the county card. **Seminole County** (November 2, 2026) uses the [county’s own Veterans Services page](https://www.seminolecountyfl.gov/departments-services/community-services/veterans-services): main office at 520 W. Lake Mary Blvd, Suite 102, Sanford; walk-ins Monday, Tuesday and Thursday mornings; outreach at VFW Post 243 in Oviedo (1st and 3rd Wednesday) and VFW Post 5405 in Winter Springs (1st Wednesday). County pages disagree on the Winter Springs schedule, so the current main page is used and visitors are told to confirm. This comes from staged research in draft PR #72. The other 66 counties are filled from [FDVA’s April 2026 county directory](https://floridavets.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CVSO-Directory-Apr-2026.pdf) (reviewed November 2, 2026): street or mailing address, published hours, a general office email where one is printed, and other office locations. Staff names and personal staff emails are not published, and emails printed with apparent typos (Lafayette, Nassau) are left out. Every primary phone in the guide matched the April directory. The directory prints Seminole’s suite as 100; the county’s own page says 102, which is used.

## Centers for Independent Living

All 67 counties map to exactly one of Florida’s 16 Centers for Independent Living (November 2, 2026). Center names and phones come from [Florida Vocational Rehabilitation’s center list](https://beta.rehabworks.org/rehabworks/adult-programs/il-plan.html); counties served and office addresses come from the [ILRU national CIL directory](https://www.ilru.org/cil-directory/fl). Where ILRU shows a different local number (for example Coalition for Independent Living Options, Central Florida and North Central Florida), the state-published phone is used. Four centers list only `http://` websites, so their cards link to the state list and print the website in the next step. Centers appear in the local-help, accessibility, care and senior daily-support and accessibility views, limited to the selected county.

## Civil legal aid

Twenty-four civil legal aid providers are mapped to the counties they serve, from [Funding Florida Legal Aid’s program chart](https://fundingfla.org/?p=24389) (page modified October 20, 2026; reviewed November 2, 2026). Every county has at least one provider. The list keeps general civil legal aid programs plus Stetson University’s Veterans Law Institute (Hillsborough), and leaves out immigration-only, children-only, post-conviction and law-school clinic programs. Phones are included only where the chart lists one; `http://` websites are printed in the next step, and the card links to the chart. Legal aid appears in the local-help, rights and housing views, limited to the selected county.

## Veteran housing (SSVF)

VA’s FY 2027 SSVF award list (supplied by the maintainer; [VA awards page](https://department.va.gov/homeless/fy-2027-grant-awards/)) names 13 Florida grantees but not the counties each serves. The housing view keeps one statewide card listing all 13, with the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans (877-424-3838) and 211 as the fallback.

Sixteen county-limited provider cards (`fl-ssvf-*`) add the grantee that serves each county where a public page says so. Volunteers of America has four office cards. Service areas come from search excerpts of grantee pages, county or city government pages, or VA's earlier award lists, so these records are marked `official_search_extract_only`, not page-reviewed. Phones are listed only where a grantee or government page printed one; otherwise the card routes through 877-424-3838 or 211.

Caveats kept on the cards:
- St. Vincent de Paul CARES lists 19 counties as its agency-wide area, not specifically SSVF.
- Changing Homelessness is mapped to Duval, Clay and Nassau. VA's earlier award list also showed St. Johns, Volusia and Flagler, which is noted on the card.
- Hearts & Homes for Veterans (Lee) and Housing for Homeless (Brevard) are new 2027 grantees whose full areas are not published.
- Thirteen counties have no county-specific card and rely on the statewide card: Baker, Bay, Calhoun, Dixie, Flagler, Glades, Gulf, Holmes, Jackson, Okeechobee, St. Johns, Volusia and Washington.

## Exceptions and quality controls

The FDVA directory shows shared county veteran-service arrangements for Jefferson/Madison and Bradford/Union; preserve their attribution rather than describing separate local staffing. The Northwest ombudsman number differs between Elder Affairs pages; the dedicated ombudsman program page is used as the primary publication. The dedicated ombudsman page prints **Oceola**, normalized to **Osceola** in the county map. The detailed district page maps that county to South Central.

Contacts and service regions are source-reviewed, not telephone-confirmed. Intake hours, current staff, eligibility, funding, device availability and placement openings require confirmation. Public data must not imply agency endorsement, formal representation or guaranteed assistance.
