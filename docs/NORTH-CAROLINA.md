# North Carolina referral edition

Published October 20, 2026. The 13th state covers all 100 counties with 79 resource records: 33 shared federal/national resources, 14 state programs, 16 aging agencies and 16 ombudsman regions. County veterans office contacts are separate from resource records.

The former Isothermal and Triangle J names remain explained alongside the current Foothills and Central Pines names. Tribal service eligibility is not inferred from county membership.

## Practical limits

- Contacts are published-source referrals, not telephone confirmations. Most county veterans contacts have not been independently rechecked on every county website; their 2024–2025 source period is visible.
- Options counseling is a local service for people age 60+ and their representatives, not a verified all-age statewide ADRC hotline. It and caregiver support use county aging contacts rather than invented statewide intake numbers.
- The NCDHHS Customer Service Center is labeled general routing. NC 211 provides broader community navigation.
- CAP/DA requires its own financial, disability, care-level and NC Medicaid Direct rules. Policy 3K-2 (amended September 1, 2025) requires assessment, service-plan documentation and authorization. It excludes new-construction modifications and nonportable alterations to rented homes.
- As reviewed October 20, 2026, Independent Living has paused new/additional purchased services since August 1, 2026, with all four priority categories closed. Applications can still be processed for the waiting list; this is not a promise of ramp funding.
- Fayetteville State Veterans Home is closed for replacement according to its official page, despite remaining linked from the homes index. The guide explicitly notes this.
- Local providers, veterans posts, senior centers, hours, waitlists, funding balances and individual admissions are not exhaustively mapped.
