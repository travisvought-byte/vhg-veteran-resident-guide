# Public resource guide

The root index.html is the GitHub Pages entry point. It embeds the 66-resource projection so search works without a server; prototype-data.json contains the matching projection.

## October 8, 2026 usability update

- Four county veterans offices are shown before the directory, with click-to-call links and contact evidence labels.
- Ten useful starting points appear by default: pension, Aid and Attendance, survivor benefits, VA enrollment, resident rights, Medicaid coordination and aging support.
- Common-need shortcuts cover benefits and care costs, surviving spouses, VA care and caregivers, resident rights and local support. All 66 records remain searchable.
- A printable staff/family handoff covers service identification, agreed referral, intake questions and follow-up without collecting resident information.
- The county selection also narrows the office cards. Statewide and federal resources remain included.
- Public cards omit internal schema/editor notes. The underlying working research is unchanged.
