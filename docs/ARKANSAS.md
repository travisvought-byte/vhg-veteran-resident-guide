# Arkansas

Reviewed November 23, 2026. Arkansas moves from a starter edition to a full edition covering all 75 counties. The edition is built from one research sheet, `data/source/states/ar.json`, by `scripts/make_county_state.py AR`; the county officers come from the first harvest of the [resource-mining pipeline](MINING.md), `data/mining/harvest/ar-adva-cvso-directory-2026-11-23.json`.

## Sources

- **County veterans service officers.** The Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs' VSO, DVSO, CVSO Directory, dated November 9, 2026 (https://www.veterans.arkansas.gov/s/Directory-11-09-2026.pdf), linked from the department's Veteran Services Map. The PDF text was extracted and read in full. 71 counties show their officer's phone and days as printed. Officers' names, emails, cell and fax numbers are left out. Addresses are printed in the directory but are not shown, because the extracted text does not tie them to their counties.
- **Four counties use the department's line, 501-683-2382.** Phillips is printed with blank phone and hours. Fulton, Montgomery and Ouachita have no phone beside their names in the extracted text; unlabeled numbers near them (870-895-3900, 870-867-3033, 870-837-2216) are not assigned until the PDF is read visually. An unlabeled Friday number after Poinsett (501-408-5579) is also left out.
- **Shared phones.** The directory prints one phone for Independence and Izard, and one (870-530-5899) for Lonoke, Monroe and Prairie on different days.
- **Area Agencies on Aging and local ombudsmen.** The Arkansas residents' rights brochure from the National Long-Term Care Ombudsman Resource Center (https://ltcombudsman.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/arkansas-residents-rights-brochure.pdf), read in full: eight agencies, every county, and eleven local ombudsman contacts. "Woodfuff" and "Montogomery" are printed for Woodruff and Montgomery. The aging cards show the phone the brochure prints for each agency; Western Arkansas uses 479-783-4500 from the agency's own site (agingwest.org) and Southwest Arkansas uses its main line, 870-234-7410. The state program's number, 501-682-1001, is printed without a label.
- **Independent living.** ILRU's Arkansas CIL directory (https://www.ilru.org/cil-directory/ar), read in full: four centers covering 25 counties.
- **Statewide cards.** Choices in Living Resource Center 866-801-3435 (page summary only), SHIP national locator (carried from the starter), Legal Aid of Arkansas helpline 800-952-9243 (apply page, read in full), the Adult Maltreatment Hotline 800-482-8049 (page summary only), and VA Central Arkansas and Ozarks health care contact pages (read in full).
