What's changing (checked 2026-11-08)

Federal special education oversight is moving

In June 2026 the U.S. Department of Education announced agreements moving day-to-day administration of special education programs to the Department of Health and Human Services and civil rights enforcement to the Department of Justice. The law itself, including your child's right to a free appropriate public education and an IEP, has not changed. Your school district and state remain responsible for services.

Sources: The federal role in special education: 5 questions (June 24, 2026) · Education Department shifting some responsibility for special education programs (June 17, 2026)

TRICARE autism coverage is under review

A National Academies study recommended replacing the Autism Care Demonstration with ABA as a regular TRICARE benefit, and senators have urged the Defense Department to act on it. Check TRICARE's page for the current rules before you enroll.

Sources: Senators' letter on the National Academies ABA study (June 26, 2026) · TRICARE Autism Care Demonstration

    Good to know

    • Ask your public school district for a free evaluation in writing if your child is 3 or older, even if your child goes to a private school or is homeschooled. Keep a copy and note the date.
    • A medical diagnosis and school eligibility are separate decisions. A child can get an IEP or 504 plan without a particular diagnosis.
    • This guide lists free public programs and nonprofits. It does not list private therapy providers, and listing a program does not mean VHG endorses or partners with it.
    • Contacts come from published official sources and were not confirmed by phone. If something is wrong, use "Report a problem" on the listing.
