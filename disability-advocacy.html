Parts, tools and repair choice

Repair and adapt equipment

The Mobility Independence Foundation (MIF) promotes open-source mobility designs that users and technicians can repair, adapt and improve. This is an advocacy goal, not a statement that every device already has a legal right to repair.

Policy requests and sources

Proposed state request

Support practical policies in your state that give mobility-equipment users and qualified technicians timely access to parts, repair information and diagnostic tools, with appropriate safeguards. Include users in decisions about repair, adaptation and reuse.

Proposed federal request

Support federal policies that improve timely access to mobility-equipment parts, repair information and diagnostic tools. Include equipment users in decisions about repair, adaptation and reuse.

Coverage, approvals and repairs

Get usable mobility equipment

Medicare Part B covers wheelchairs and scooters when its requirements are met. Power devices require medical necessity, an examination and a prescription. Medicare rules are separate from each state’s Medicaid rules.

Policy requests and sources

Proposed state request

Improve Medicaid access in your state to medically necessary mobility equipment and repairs. Ask for transparent authorization timelines, understandable denial notices, clear appeal routes and a workable plan when essential equipment is unusable.

Proposed federal request

Improve Medicare access to medically necessary mobility equipment and repairs. Ask for transparent authorization timelines, understandable denial notices, clear appeal routes and a workable plan when essential equipment is unusable.

Ramps, entrances and modifications

Make home accessible

State programs can pay for home changes. In Ohio, for example, Home Care Waiver rules list home modification among covered services. A listing does not establish an individual’s eligibility or approval. Housing accessibility also involves different federal laws, depending on the housing and situation.

Policy requests and sources

Proposed state request

Improve access to home-modification programs in your state, including clear application steps, timely assessments and coordination before discharge. Help people find the right funding route for ramps, entrances and accessible bathrooms.

Proposed federal request

Support accessible housing and home-modification funding that helps people remain at home. Improve coordination across housing and disability programs so people can find an appropriate application route.

Accessible transit and reliable rides

Travel and participate

FTA publishes guidance on wheelchair access, paratransit and other ADA transportation duties. It also accepts public-transit discrimination complaints and encourages people to first contact the transit provider.

Policy requests and sources

Proposed state request

Support reliable accessible transportation in your state, including rural service, usable stops and clear paratransit application information. Ask transit agencies to involve riders with disabilities in planning and publish accessible complaint routes.

Proposed federal request

Support reliable accessible public transportation and oversight of disability access requirements. Include riders with disabilities in planning and improve access to clear paratransit information and complaint routes.

Personal assistance and daily support

Choose community living

DOJ explains that the ADA’s integration mandate can apply to people in institutions and people at serious risk of institutionalization. Services and eligibility depend on the individual situation; a policy letter is not a service application.

Policy requests and sources

Proposed state request

Strengthen home- and community-based services, personal assistance and transition coordination in your state. Include people with disabilities in planning, improve continuity of support and make program information and participation accessible.

Proposed federal request

Strengthen home- and community-based services and community integration. Support a stable personal-assistance workforce, continuity of support and meaningful participation by people with disabilities in program decisions.
