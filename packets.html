Printable resource packets

Paper lists of the first offices to call, for people who do not use the internet: nursing home residents, older adults without a smartphone, and people in jail or prison. For social workers, activity directors, chaplains, County Veterans Service Offices, libraries and jail law libraries.

Ohio: pick a county

Each county packet is two letter-size pages: the county veterans office, the closest VA care, the Area Agency on Aging, the long-term care ombudsman, Adult Protective Services, free legal help and crisis lines, with a QR code to the online guide.

Printing tips

  • Print at 100% or Actual size. Black and white is fine.
  • County packets are two pages: print both sides of one sheet if you can.
  • A number is wrong? Use Report a problem in the guide, or email travis@vethomeguard.org. Please do not include anyone’s private details.
