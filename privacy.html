PRIVACY

Privacy

How the Veteran Resource Guide handles information about your visit. Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name.
  • We count visits without cookies and without keeping personal information.
  • The state, county and help you choose stay on your own device and in the page address.
  • The maps’ pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which sees your internet address and the area on the map.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Counting visits

We use GoatCounter to count page views, so we know which pages people use. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store personal information. For each page view it records things like the page visited, the site that sent you here, your browser, your screen size, and an approximate country worked out from your internet (IP) address. It does not store the IP address.

We leave out everything after the question mark in a page address, so your county and the help you chose are never counted.

What you choose and type

The guide works in your browser. When you pick a state, a county or a kind of help, the page shows it from lists that download with the page; nothing is sent to us. Your choices show in the page address so you can bookmark or share them.

Letters you write on the advocacy pages, checklists you tick and drafts in the guided assistance stay in your browser. We do not store or send them. You copy, print or send them yourself.

Maps and “Use my location”

The nursing home and assisted living page shows its list on a map. The map pictures (tiles) come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). To draw the map, your browser asks OpenStreetMap for the pictures of the area you look at, so OpenStreetMap sees your internet (IP) address, the area on the map and which website you are on, like any website you visit. Your browser does not send it the ZIP code you typed or your location, but the area on the map is near the place you searched. Read the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s privacy policy. The map code itself (Leaflet) comes from this site. If you hide the map, your browser remembers that on your device, and the map pictures stop loading.

“Use my location” asks your browser for your location. If you say yes, the page finds the nearest ZIP code on your own device and searches it like a typed one. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not kept.

Who hosts the site

The guide is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security. Read GitHub’s privacy statement. A few pages show a logo from a partner’s website, which receives an ordinary request for that picture.

Advertising

The guide does not show ads and has no affiliate links. When a business or group sponsors a guide to help keep it free, its spot is labeled “Sponsored”, and sponsorship never changes the free listings or their order.

Email and corrections

If you email us a correction, we get your email address and what you write. We use it to fix the guide and to reply, and we never sell or share it. Please send public resource information only, never a resident’s personal or health details.

Children

The guide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Questions

The guide is shared by Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio nonprofit. Questions about privacy: travis@vethomeguard.org.
