Ohio law and official sources

The rules, with the links.

Charitable bingo in Ohio is governed by Chapter 2915 of the Revised Code and licensed by the Attorney General's Charitable Law Section.

These are short notes for orientation. They are not legal advice, and the law changes. Read the linked source, and call the Charitable Law Section at 800-282-0515 or your attorney about your own situation.

The law itself

  • Ohio Revised Code Chapter 2915 Gambling, including all charitable bingo statutes.
  • ORC 2915.01: definitions Bingo, bingo game operator, expenses, charitable purpose, distributor.

How Ohio Bingo stays inside the rules

  • We list and promote games. We do not conduct bingo, work sessions, or handle any hall's money.
  • Our paid work for halls is advertising. We do not charge consulting fees for services related to a bingo session.
  • We take no commission, referral fee or rebate on bingo supplies.
  • Listings are free to every hall whether or not it works with us.
