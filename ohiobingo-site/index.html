Why a veterans nonprofit runs this

Veteran Home Guardians exists so that no veteran is forgotten in a nursing home. Bingo is one of the few regular outings that gets residents out of their rooms and back among neighbors.

Promotion work and sponsorship on this site fund that mission: Guardian visits to veterans in long-term care, and the free resource guide for veterans and families.
