The $100 hall visit.

We come to you. You get promotion you can print, post and share. One flat price, no contract.

Request a visit

What $100 buys

  • A visit to your hall by someone who ran and promoted bingo at the Marengo American Legion for three years.
  • Photos of your hall for your listing and your own use.
  • A finished flyer for your game, ready to print and post.
  • A one-page game sheet: when, where, what it costs and what it funds. See a sample.
  • A complete listing in the Ohio Bingo Finder: days, times, cost, contact, parking and step-free entry.
  • A short promotion plan: where to post, who to tell, and which nearby care facilities and senior groups to invite.
$100flat

How it works

  1. Request a visit with the short form below.
  2. We call to set a date. Visits are scheduled by region.
  3. We visit, ideally on a game day, and gather what we need.
  4. You receive your flyer, game sheet and updated listing, with an itemized invoice and receipt for your records.

What the $100 is, and is not

The $100 pays for advertising your game: the visit, the photos and the finished materials. Ohio lets a charity spend bingo receipts on advertising at customary and reasonable amounts (ORC 2915.09).

It is not a consulting fee. Ohio does not let a charity running traditional bingo pay consulting fees for services related to its session. We do not work your session, handle your money, or charge for advice about how you run your game.

While we are there, ask us anything. Conversation is free, and we will point you to the Attorney General's forms and training.

Payment by check or electronic transfer. If you are unsure whether this fits your organization, ask the Charitable Law Section at 800-282-0515 or your attorney first. This page is not legal advice.
