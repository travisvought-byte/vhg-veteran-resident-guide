Your first night

  1. Call ahead. Schedules change for holidays and weather. Every listing has a phone number when the hall has shared one.
  2. Come early. Doors usually open well before the first game so you can buy your cards and find a seat.
  3. Bring cash and a dauber. Many halls are cash only. Most sell daubers at the door.
  4. Ask a volunteer. The people running the game are unpaid volunteers. They will gladly explain the games.

Good to know

You must be 18

The Ohio Attorney General's office says players must be at least 18.
