For providers

Providers sign up here

A free listing for people and businesses who help veterans at home in Morrow and Knox counties: repairs, accessibility changes, caregiving, and yard and snow work. A veteran vouches for you, customers call you directly, and you never pay for a lead.

How it works

  1. Send us the form below.
  2. We call the veteran who vouched for you, and check any license and insurance.
  3. We send you your listing to approve.
  4. It goes live on Find help at home. Nothing goes up before you approve it.

Repair contractor anywhere in Ohio? The veteran-owned contractor directory lists you statewide, too.

The rules

  1. A veteran vouches for you

    Name a veteran who has hired you or worked beside you. We call them to hear about your work before anything goes up. You don’t have to be a veteran yourself; if your business is veteran-owned, your listing says so.

  2. Direct contact, never lead fees

    Customers call you. The listing is free, there is no fee per call or per job, and we never sell or share anyone’s name or number.

  3. Your rates, in public

    Publish your normal rate, any trip or service fee and what it covers, and price ranges for two or three common jobs. You set your prices, not us. Our fair-price ranges are a reference for customers, not a cap.

  4. No surprises

    • Say what you charge before you start, including any trip fee. For repairs, Ohio law already requires it.
    • For repair and remodeling work, give a written estimate before you start, and get the customer’s OK before the bill goes more than 10 percent (or $5) over it.
    • Give an itemized bill or receipt.
    • Only take work you’re trained, licensed and insured to do.

  5. Feedback from real jobs

    Customers tell us how a job went, with the date and the work done. We send it to you and ask how you made any problem right. What we can confirm goes on your listing. A pattern of problems that aren’t made right takes it down.

  6. Licensed and insured where the work needs it

    Give us any state license or city or county registration your work needs, and your insurance. Your listing shows what we checked and when.
