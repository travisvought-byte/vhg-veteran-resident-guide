Bingo in Kansas

Groups and places in Kansas with a current state license or permit for bingo, by county. The state list does not show game days, so call before you go.

Shows bingo near you in every state we cover, closest first.

Kansas licenses bingo through the Department of Revenue, which publishes a list of licensed bingo organizations with their addresses and phone numbers. Kansas bingo is limited to nonprofit religious, charitable, fraternal, educational and veterans organizations licensed by the Department of Revenue, 785-368-8222.

Kansas's list names organizations licensed for bingo, with the address and phone the state gives for each. It does not show game days or times, or say where each group plays, so call before you go. Organizations the list shows only with a post office box are not shown here; the state list has them.
