Bingo in New Mexico

Groups and places in New Mexico with a current state license or permit for bingo, by county. The state list does not show game days, so call before you go.

Shows bingo near you in every state we cover, closest first.

New Mexico's Gaming Control Board lists the groups licensed for bingo, raffles or pull-tabs, with their addresses and phone numbers. New Mexico bingo is overseen by the New Mexico Gaming Control Board, 505-841-9700.
