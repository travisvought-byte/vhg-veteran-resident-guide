Bingo in Mississippi

Groups and places in Mississippi with a current state license or permit for bingo, by county. The state list does not show game days, so call before you go.

Shows bingo near you in every state we cover, closest first.

Mississippi licenses charitable bingo through the Mississippi Gaming Commission, which keeps a map of licensed bingo halls with their license dates. Mississippi charitable bingo is licensed by the Mississippi Gaming Commission.

Mississippi's map names halls licensed for bingo. It does not show game days or times, so call before you go. Halls whose license on the map has passed its end date are not shown.
