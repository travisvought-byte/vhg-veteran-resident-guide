Where these lists come from

Florida

Florida does not license bingo statewide, so there is no state list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. Under Florida law, charities, veterans' groups and some homeowners' and mobile home park associations may run bingo; some counties, such as Pinellas, also license it locally.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Florida law on bingo (section 849.0931) ↗

Indiana

The Indiana Gaming Commission lists groups with a current license that allows bingo. Its list does not show game days or times. Indiana bingo is licensed by the Indiana Gaming Commission's Charity Gaming Division, 317-232-4646.

Checked November 15, 2026 (list dated November 13, 2026). Sources: Indiana Gaming Commission: Qualified Organizations with Current Licenses ↗ · Indiana Gaming Commission, Charity Gaming ↗

Kentucky

We haven't found a public list of Kentucky bingo games yet, so we're building one with help from halls and players. Kentucky bingo is licensed by the Office of Charitable Gaming, now part of Kentucky Horse Racing & Gaming.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Kentucky Office of Charitable Gaming ↗

Massachusetts

In Massachusetts, bingo is called beano and is licensed by the State Lottery Commission. We haven't found a public list of game days yet, so we're building one with help from halls and players. Massachusetts beano is licensed by the Massachusetts State Lottery Commission, 781-849-5555.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: Massachusetts: questions about nonprofit gaming events ↗

Michigan

Michigan's Charitable Gaming Division lists licensed weekly bingo games with the day and hours. Michigan bingo is licensed by the Michigan Lottery's Charitable Gaming Division, 517-335-5780.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Michigan Charitable Gaming Division: Find a Weekly Bingo ↗ · Michigan Charitable Gaming Division ↗

Missouri

Missouri licenses bingo statewide through the Missouri Gaming Commission. We haven't found a public list of game days yet, so we're building one with help from halls and players. Missouri bingo is licensed by the Missouri Gaming Commission's Charitable Games Division. Only licensed religious, charitable, fraternal, veteran and service organizations may run it.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: Missouri Gaming Commission: Charitable Games Division ↗

New York

New York bingo licenses come from each city, town or village, so there is no statewide list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. In New York, a group first gets a bingo identification number from the State Gaming Commission's Division of Charitable Gaming, and the license itself comes from the local city, town or village clerk.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: New York State Gaming Commission: Charitable Gaming ↗

Ohio

Ohio has its own free bingo finder with game days and times, from the same team. Ohio bingo is licensed by the Ohio Attorney General.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Ohio Attorney General: authorized bingo locations ↗ · Ohio Bingo Finder ↗

Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania bingo licenses come from each county, so there is no statewide list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. In Pennsylvania, bingo licenses are issued by each county treasurer's office.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: How county bingo licenses work (Cumberland County example) ↗

Texas

Texas licenses charitable bingo statewide, and the state runs its own bingo hall finder. We have not loaded Texas games here yet, so use the state's finder for now. Texas bingo was regulated by the Texas Lottery Commission until 2025, when the Legislature moved it to the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. The bingo line is 800-246-4677.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: Texas charitable bingo (txbingo.org) ↗

Utah

Utah does not license bingo, and state law does not allow gambling. The bingo we list in Utah is free bingo at senior centers. We're building the list with help from centers and players. Utah has no bingo regulator. State law prohibits gambling, so the places listed here offer bingo free of charge.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: Utah Code: gambling (section 76-9-1402) ↗

West Virginia

We haven't found a public list of West Virginia bingo games yet, so we're building one with help from halls and players. West Virginia bingo is licensed by the State Tax Commissioner, 304-558-3333.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: West Virginia Tax Division: bingo and raffle licenses ↗
