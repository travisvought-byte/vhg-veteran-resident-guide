Where these lists come from

Florida

Florida does not license bingo statewide, so there is no state list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. Under Florida law, charities, veterans' groups and some homeowners' and mobile home park associations may run bingo; some counties, such as Pinellas, also license it locally.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Florida law on bingo (section 849.0931) ↗

Indiana

The Indiana Gaming Commission lists groups with a current license that allows bingo. Its list does not show game days or times. Indiana bingo is licensed by the Indiana Gaming Commission's Charity Gaming Division, 317-232-4646.

Checked November 15, 2026 (list dated November 13, 2026). Sources: Indiana Gaming Commission: Qualified Organizations with Current Licenses ↗ · Indiana Gaming Commission, Charity Gaming ↗

Kentucky

We haven't found a public list of Kentucky bingo games with their days and times, so we're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. Kentucky bingo is licensed by the Office of Charitable Gaming, now part of Kentucky Horse Racing & Gaming.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: Kentucky Office of Charitable Gaming ↗

Michigan

Michigan's Charitable Gaming Division lists licensed weekly bingo games with the day and hours. Michigan bingo is licensed by the Michigan Lottery's Charitable Gaming Division, 517-335-5780.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Michigan Charitable Gaming Division: Find a Weekly Bingo ↗ · Michigan Charitable Gaming Division ↗

Mississippi

Mississippi licenses charitable bingo through the Mississippi Gaming Commission, which keeps a map of licensed bingo halls with their license dates. Mississippi charitable bingo is licensed by the Mississippi Gaming Commission.

Checked November 17, 2026 (list dated September 29, 2026). Sources: Mississippi map of charitable gaming licensees ↗ · Mississippi Gaming Commission: Charitable Gaming ↗

Missouri

Missouri licenses bingo statewide through the Missouri Gaming Commission, and the Commission's Find Local Bingo Games page lists each game with its day and start time. Missouri bingo is licensed by the Missouri Gaming Commission's Charitable Games Division. Only licensed religious, charitable, fraternal, veteran and service organizations may run it.

Checked November 19, 2026. Sources: Missouri Gaming Commission: Find Local Bingo Games ↗ · Missouri Gaming Commission: Charitable Games Division ↗

Montana

Montana allows live bingo under permits from the state Gambling Control Division, which publishes a list of places that offer live keno or bingo. Many are bars, casinos and clubs. Montana live bingo permits come from the Department of Justice's Gambling Control Division, 406-444-1971.

Checked November 17, 2026 (list dated October 30, 2026). Sources: Montana list of places offering live keno or bingo (PDF) ↗ · Montana Department of Justice: Gambling Control Division ↗

New Mexico

New Mexico's Gaming Control Board lists the groups licensed for bingo, raffles or pull-tabs, with their addresses and phone numbers. New Mexico bingo is overseen by the New Mexico Gaming Control Board, 505-841-9700.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: New Mexico map of bingo, raffle and pull-tab locations ↗ · New Mexico Gaming Control Board ↗

New York

New York bingo licenses come from each city, town or village, so there is no statewide list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. In New York, a group first gets a bingo identification number from the State Gaming Commission's Division of Charitable Gaming, and the license itself comes from the local city, town or village clerk.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: New York State Gaming Commission: Charitable Gaming ↗

Ohio

Ohio has its own free bingo finder with game days and times, from the same team. Ohio bingo is licensed by the Ohio Attorney General.

Checked November 15, 2026. Sources: Ohio Attorney General: authorized bingo locations ↗ · Ohio Bingo Finder ↗

Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania bingo licenses come from each county, so there is no statewide list of games. We're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. In Pennsylvania, bingo licenses are issued by each county treasurer's office.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: How county bingo licenses work (Cumberland County example) ↗

Texas

Texas licenses charitable bingo statewide, and the state's Bingo Hall Locator lists each licensed hall with its days and times. Texas bingo was regulated by the Texas Lottery Commission until 2025, when the Legislature moved it to the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. The bingo line is 800-246-4677.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: Texas Bingo Hall Locator ↗ · Texas charitable bingo (txbingo.org) ↗

Utah

Utah does not license bingo, and state law does not allow gambling. The bingo we list in Utah is free bingo at senior centers. We're building the list with help from centers and players. Utah has no bingo regulator. State law prohibits gambling, so the places listed here offer bingo free of charge.

Checked November 16, 2026. Sources: Utah Code: gambling (section 76-9-1402) ↗

Virginia

Virginia's Office of Charitable and Regulatory Programs publishes a directory of bingo sessions, with the days and the times the doors open and close. Games marked "in the social quarters" may be for members and their guests, so call first. Virginia charitable gaming permits come from the Office of Charitable and Regulatory Programs at the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services. Its complaint line is 804-371-0498.

Checked November 17, 2026 (list dated September 1, 2026). Sources: Virginia directory of bingo sessions (PDF) ↗ · Virginia Office of Charitable and Regulatory Programs: Charitable Gaming ↗

West Virginia

We haven't found a public list of West Virginia bingo games with their days and times, so we're building one from places that publish their own schedules, with help from halls and players. West Virginia bingo is licensed by the State Tax Commissioner, 304-558-3333.

Checked November 17, 2026. Sources: West Virginia Tax Division: bingo and raffle licenses ↗

Every other state

Each of these states has its own page. It names who licenses or oversees bingo there, links the state’s own list or finder where there is one, shows its sources, and gives a free way to send us game times: Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, North Carolina, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Oregon, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Vermont, Washington, Wisconsin, Wyoming.

Game days and hours can change, especially around holidays. Call ahead before you go, and ask about parking, steps and seating if that matters for you.
