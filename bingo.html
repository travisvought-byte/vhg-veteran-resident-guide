Bingo and community activities

Places to connect, enjoy a game and ask about a group outing. Find charitable bingo sessions and senior-center activities separately. Military service is not required; each venue has its own attendance rules.

Initial coverage: Morrow, Knox, Delaware and Marion

Six researched venues, not a complete statewide directory. Cardington and Marengo bingo activity is confirmed firsthand by Travis Vought; that does not confirm current times, prices, accessibility or licenses.

Statewide license import is pending. The Attorney General report was visible through indexed retrieval, but direct downloads were blocked on October 8, 2026. No statewide venue count or license status is asserted here.

Before planning an outing

Confirm the next session, total cost, age and guest rules, step-free access, accessible restrooms, parking, companion attendance and any registration requirements. Accessibility has not yet been verified for these venues.

Growing across Ohio

The license report can identify traditional bingo locations. Type I is traditional bingo; Type II covers instant gaming at bingo sessions; Type III covers instant gaming outside sessions. Pull-tab or electronic gaming locations alone are not evidence of a scheduled traditional bingo event. Senior-center activities are tracked separately; their license requirements have not been assessed here.
