Repair it. Adapt it. Keep it useful.

  • Repair: access to parts, service information, diagnostics and a choice of repair provider.
  • Adapt: equipment that can be customized to a person’s changing needs, with appropriate technical support.
  • Reuse: repairable equipment that can continue helping someone rather than being discarded.

These are related goals. A repair campaign may address only some of them; its scope matters.

Explore the Mobility Independence Foundation: MIF develops and shares open-source mobility designs and promotes users’ ability to repair, adapt and improve their equipment.
